Privacy Policy — SOME Cheerleading

Effective date: November 26, 2026 · Last updated: November 26, 2026

SOME Cheerleading is a cheerleading education app — the front door for anyone who wants to become a cheerleader, and for their parents: what cheer is, the truth about getting in, the positions, skills in order, tryouts, the roads, honest costs, and safety first. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores — on your device only

The app keeps only what you put in it: your My Plan worksheet answers, your tryout checklist check-offs, and your Premium subscription state (as the App Store reports it to the app). All of it is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network calls at all — and it disappears when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No chat, no messaging, no comments, and no sharing of anything you type. There is no way to contact other users through this app.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location. (The one photograph in the app came bundled with it — a U.S. Navy photo in the public domain.)

Purchases

The only purchase the app offers is its own Premium subscription (SOME Cheerleading Premium: $4.99/month or $49/year). If you subscribe, the transaction is processed by the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The App Store's own privacy policy governs purchase data it handles.

Children and families

The app is designed for families — aspiring cheerleaders and their parents. Its content is family-safe educational material, including a standing safety curriculum. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children: no names, no accounts, no messages, nothing. Parents can delete everything the app holds on a device at any time from the My Plan page, or by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
