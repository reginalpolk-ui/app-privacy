AIZI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

AIZI is a curated showcase of tested AI powers, published by Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc. — four plain-language powers, the Certified Shelf of tested picks, and information about AIZI Agents for organizations. AIZI runs under a published rule we call the No-Data-Sale Law: conversation data is never sold, and never used for training without opt-in consent. This policy says exactly what happens to your information in this build — and, just as plainly, what will change in the production release.

What this build does with your information

  • The showcase runs on your device. In this build, the four Powers (Explain It Plain, Write It Right, Plan It, Read the Fine Print) run simple local versions on your device, working with the built-in sample content and the text you type or paste in. That text is processed on the device and is not sent to us or to any model provider from this build.
  • Live model routing is not switched on in this build. Each Power names the certified model that will do the work in production — that naming is a design label, printed next to the Power. This build contains no API keys and makes no model calls.
  • We collect nothing. AIZI collects no personal data — no name, no email address, no location, no device identifiers, no usage history. There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising. The app contains no analytics tools, no trackers, and no ads, and it does not follow you across other apps or websites.
  • One ordinary exception: the app's pages load typefaces from Google Fonts when the device is online. That request carries no text you typed and no information about you beyond what any web request carries (such as an IP address, handled under Google's own policy). Everything else the app shows you is bundled inside the app itself.
  • Nothing is stored about you. Text you enter into a Power stays in the app's memory for that session; AIZI keeps no copy on our servers, because this build sends us nothing at all.

When live routing is on (the production release)

  • When a production version enables live routing, the text you submit to a Power is sent to the model provider named on that Power's door (for example, Anthropic's Muse API) so it can produce the answer, and the answer is returned to your device. That is the only way the feature can work, and we say so here before it ships.
  • Before that version ships, this policy will be updated to name the actual providers in use and their retention terms, and the app will say the same thing next to each Power.
  • Even then: what you submit is never sold, never used to train models without your opt-in consent, and AIZI does not keep voiceprints of anyone. If a future version adds accounts, saved documents, or purchases, this policy will describe those flows before that version ships.

AIZI Agents (for organizations)

  • The app describes AIZI Agents — after-hours and front-desk voice and chat agents sold to organizations. The app itself does not enroll anyone in an agent service and collects no organization or caller information.
  • Agent deployments run under their own agreements with the organization. By design and by our published rules, those agents disclose that they are AI at the start of every interaction, announce recording, never enroll or store voiceprints, and route crisis signals to human help first.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data. Not to anyone, not ever.
  • We never train on your content without opt-in consent.
  • We never ask for passwords, payment information, or government ID numbers anywhere in this app. AIZI Premium, when offered, is billed by the App Store — we never see your card.

Your control

  • Because this build collects and transmits nothing about you, there is no information about you on our servers to view, correct, or delete.
  • Removing the app from your device removes the app. That is the whole story for this build.
  • Questions: reginalpolk@luvvets.com

Kids

  • AIZI is a general-audience productivity app. It is not a companion or friend app, it has no relationship-simulating personas, and it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date before it takes effect. The change that matters most — live model routing — is described above in advance, and this policy will be updated again when it ships.
