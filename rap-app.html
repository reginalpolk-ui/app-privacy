RAP APP — CONTRACT PROTECTION

Rap App Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Rap App teaches artists what music contracts mean and screens their own contracts for red flags. A contract is some of the most sensitive paper an artist holds. This policy says exactly what this version of the app stores, where, and what it never does. The short version: your contract never leaves your phone, and it is never stored — not by the app, not anywhere.

What this app stores, and where

  • On your device only: your Protect Your Publishing checklist progress, a count of which Contract X-Rays you have opened, a record of which Deep Dive reports you have unlocked (restored from the App Store and kept on-device so the reports open offline), and — when you screen a contract — an anonymous count record: the type of deal the screen identified and how many flags of each severity it found. All of it is saved in your browser's local storage so the app remembers it between visits.
  • Nothing is transmitted. This version has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no server that receives anything. Even the screening counts stay on the device in this version.

Screening a contract — the custody promise

  • Your contract's words and photos are never uploaded and never stored. The screening engine runs entirely on your device, in memory. When the screen finishes, the text is discarded; the only thing kept is the anonymous count described above — never the text, never a quote, never a photo, never a filename.
  • Photos: when you photograph contract pages, the app reads them on the device with the open-source Tesseract OCR engine. To do that, on your explicit tap it downloads the engine and its English language data from the jsDelivr content-delivery network (and, for a scanned PDF, the PDF.js page renderer from cdnjs). Those downloads are requests for software only — they carry none of your information. The reading itself happens on your device, and before anything is screened, the app shows you the reading and asks you to confirm it.
  • PDFs: a PDF's text layer is extracted on the device. A scanned PDF's pages are rendered and read on the device by the same OCR flow. Nothing about the file leaves the phone.

What we never do — no exceptions

  • We never sell artist data. Not to anyone, not ever, not in any form.
  • We never take a percentage of an artist's royalties and never take label or distributor money that steers the teaching.
  • We never share what you screen. There is nothing to share: screened contracts are not stored or transmitted in the first place.
  • Attorney listings in Find a Lawyer, when they open, are labeled advertising. They sit outside the teaching, are never ranked by payment beyond placement in that section, and Rap App never takes a cut of any attorney's fees.

What we do not collect

  • No email address or account is required to use the app. No location. No contacts. No photos or files are collected or kept (contract photos are read in memory and discarded — see above). No payment information: the two optional Deep Dive purchases are handled entirely by the App Store through your Apple ID — Rap App never sees, receives, or stores your payment details. The app keeps only an on-device record of which Deep Dives you have unlocked, so the reports open offline; that record is erased with the erase control below, and your purchases themselves live with Apple and restore with your Apple ID.

Security — stated plainly

  • In this version, your progress data is protected by never leaving the device: there is no account to breach and no server copy to steal. What that means in practice: whoever holds the unlocked device can open the app and see your checklist progress and screening counts (never your contracts — those are gone the moment a screen finishes). Use your device's lock screen, and erase the app's stored data (below) when you are done with a device.
  • Any future version that syncs anything anywhere will ship only after an independent security review, and this policy will be rewritten first to state its protections exactly.

Your control

  • You can erase everything the app stored — checklist progress, X-Ray counts, screening counts, and your Deep Dive unlock record — at any time with "Erase everything Rap App stored on this device" on the Home or Pricing tab. Because the data only ever existed on the device, deletion there is complete. (Erasing the unlock record does not cancel or refund a purchase: your Deep Dives stay yours with Apple and restore with your Apple ID.)
  • See the Delete Your Data page for the step-by-step.

Not legal advice

  • Rap App provides legal information and education only. It is not a law firm, and a screening result is not legal advice and never a statement that a contract is safe. Only a real entertainment lawyer can clear a real contract.

Children

  • Rap App is built for artists old enough to be offered contracts and is rated 18+. It is not directed to children.

Changes

  • If this policy changes — for example, if a synced version ships — the new version will be posted at this address with a new date before it takes effect, and the app will say plainly what changed.
