Data Deletion — SOME Politics

What data exists

SOME Politics has no accounts and no servers holding your information. The only data the app keeps is what you enter into its tools — your Supporter Book, follow-up list, “Where Do I Stand?” worksheet, calendar check-offs, and tracker counts — plus your Premium subscription state as the App Store reports it. All of it is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. We cannot see it, and we hold no copy of it. The app makes no network calls and collects nothing.

How to delete it

  1. Delete the app: removing SOME Politics from your device deletes all of its locally stored data with it — the whole Supporter Book, worksheet, follow-ups, calendar, and counters, immediately and completely.
  2. Delete a single supporter: open the Supporter Book, find the entry, and tap Delete. Follow-ups can be deleted the same way from the follow-up list, and worksheet fields can simply be cleared.
  3. Keep your list first, if you want it: with Premium, use Export the Book in the Supporter Book to copy your list as plain text before deleting — once the app is gone, the list is gone with it, by design.

Because nothing is ever transmitted to us, there is no server-side data for us to delete and no deletion request is needed. If you wrote to our support email, that email lives in our support mailbox like any other email; to have it deleted, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com from the same address and ask for its deletion — we will delete it and confirm.

App Store purchase records

Subscription purchase records are held by the App Store under its own policies; manage or delete those through your Apple account settings.
