Donate a Home Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Donate a Home is an information and donor-intake app for people who want to donate a house or another asset to benefit veterans and their children, through Stars & Stripes Children Fund and Eagles & Angels Veterans Corporation. This policy says exactly what happens to your information — in this app, the answer is: it stays on your device until you choose to send it yourself.

What this app stores, and where

  • On your device only: your donor-intake draft (your name, phone, email, the property's address, city, state, situation, the organization you chose to benefit, and your notes). If you start a "Stay in the loop" donor-list signup, that draft — your first name, email, and phone if you add it — is saved the same way. Both are saved in your browser's local storage so you can stop and come back — the same way a note app on your phone saves a draft.
  • Nothing is transmitted by the app. This app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no server that receives your answers.

When you tap "Send"

  • The Donate form does not submit anything itself. It opens your own email app with a message prefilled to reginalpolk@luvvets.com, and you press send there. That email — sent by you, from your own email account — is how the organizations receive your intake information, and they use it to follow up about your gift.
  • The same is true of the "Ask a question" and asset-gift email buttons: they open your email app; nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • The "Stay in the loop" donor-list signup works the same way: it opens your email app with a prefilled message, and you join the organizations' own donor list only when you send it. That list belongs to the organizations — it is never sold or shared — and you can leave that list at any time with a single email.
  • If you call instead, the organizations receive only what you tell them on the call.

What we never ask for in this app

  • Social Security numbers, bank logins, or card numbers. The app never asks for them. Any donation paperwork later in the process is handled directly with the organization and its facilitation partner — never through this app.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data. Not to anyone, not ever. No advertisers, no data brokers, no cross-app tracking.

Your control

  • Erase your drafts (the intake and any donor-list signup) on your device any time: Donate tab → "Erase my draft on this device." They are gone immediately and completely, because they only ever existed there.
  • You can also clear it from your browser's site-data settings. See the Delete Your Data page — including how to ask us to delete an email you sent.
  • Questions: reginalpolk@luvvets.com

Kids

  • Donate a Home is a giving-information service for adults considering a charitable gift. It is not directed at children.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date before it takes effect.
