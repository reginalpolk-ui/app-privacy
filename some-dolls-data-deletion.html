Data Deletion — SOME Dolls

Effective date: November 24, 2026

SOME Dolls collects no data. There is no account, no server copy of anything, and nothing about you ever leaves your device.

The only on-device state

The app keeps three small things in its local storage on your device: your place in the three Test Drive demos (which step you were on in each); your collection-journal records, if you keep a journal; and a flag recording whether SOME Dolls Premium is active. Nothing else is stored, and none of it ever leaves the device. (Your Premium subscription record itself is held by Apple, not by the app — manage it in your App Store account settings. Any Doll Shop order is held by the web storefront where you placed it.)

How to delete it

Delete the SOME Dolls app from your device. Its local storage is removed with it, completely and permanently. There is nothing else to delete and nothing to request from us, because we never received anything.
