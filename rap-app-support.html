RAP APP — CONTRACT PROTECTION

Rap App Support

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Rap App teaches artists what music contracts mean — and screens their own contracts for red flags before they sign. This page is how to get help with the app itself.

Contact

  • Email reginalpolk@luvvets.com and a person will answer. There is no account and no sign-in, so support never needs a password — just tell us what you were doing in the app and what happened.

Common questions

  • Does Rap App cost anything? The core is free, forever: all the education, the Contract X-Ray, and the contract screen with its summary — verdict, deal type, danger count, and most dangerous flag. Two Deep Dives are optional one-time purchases at $9.99 each: the Deal Deep Dive and the Screen My Contract full report. No subscriptions.
  • How do I restore a Deep Dive? Deep Dives are sold by Apple through the App Store and tied to your Apple ID. On any device signed in to the same Apple ID, use "Restore purchases" in the app — there is no second charge.
  • Where does my contract go when I screen it? Nowhere. The screen runs entirely on your phone, and your contract is never stored — not by the app, not anywhere. When the screen finishes, the text is discarded. See the Privacy Policy for the full statement.
  • Is a screening result legal advice? No. Rap App provides legal information and education only. It is not a law firm, a screening result is not legal advice, and no result here ever calls a contract safe. Only a real entertainment lawyer can clear a real contract — the app's Lawyer section lists the six questions to ask one.
  • How do I delete what the app saved? Use "Erase everything Rap App stored on this device" on the Home or Pricing tab. That removes the on-device items (checklist progress, X-Ray counts, and the anonymous screening counts). Contracts are never saved in the first place, so there is nothing of them to delete.
  • The screen could not read my photo. What now? You can also upload a PDF or paste the contract text — all three routes run the same on-device screen. Before anything is screened from a photo, the app shows you its reading of your pages and asks you to confirm it.

Report a problem

  • If a lesson, clause card, or screening result looks wrong, email reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the section name (X-Ray, Screen, Million-Dollar Trap, Publishing, Independent, Market, Business, Lawyer, Pricing) and what looks wrong. Corrections matter to us — artists act on this teaching.
