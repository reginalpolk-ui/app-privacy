SOME Scuba Diving — Privacy Policy

SOME Scuba Diving is published by Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc. Effective: upon publication · Contact: reginalpolk@luvvets.com

The short version

SOME Scuba Diving collects no data. Not "very little" — none. There is no account to create, no analytics running, no advertising tracker, and no server receiving anything from your phone.

What the app stores, and where

Two things are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage:

  • My start list — the readiness boxes you tick on the How To Start shelf.
  • Pressure Lab depth — the last depth you dialed into the interactive lab.

These never leave your device. They are not transmitted to Reginal Polk Technologies or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-up, no login, no email address, no phone number — ever requested.
  • No location collection. No contacts, photos, or camera access.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information — there is none to sell or share.

Links to partners

Shelves in the app may, in future versions, carry clearly labeled links ("doors") to dive schools, resorts, or gear retailers. Those sites are run by those businesses, and their own privacy policies apply once you leave the app. When a booking or purchase follows from one of those links, the business may pay Reginal Polk Technologies a commission or placement fee; that transaction happens between you and the business and involves no data from the app.

Children

SOME Scuba Diving is a general-audience educational app about a sport. It collects no data from anyone, including children. (For the record: scuba certification itself has minimum ages set by the training agencies — typically 10 for junior programs — which the app explains on its How To Start shelf.)

Changes

If a future version of SOME Scuba Diving ever changes this policy, the new policy will be posted at this address before that version ships.
