Data Deletion — I'm Telling

Effective date: November 24, 2026

The short version

I'm Telling holds no data about you on any server — there is no account and the developer receives nothing from the app. Everything the app keeps (your Tip IDs, your tip lifecycle log, and any draft in progress) is stored only on your own device, and you can delete all of it yourself, instantly.

Delete everything, in the app

Open the My Tips tab and tap "Erase everything", then tap once more to confirm. Every I'm Telling record on the device — Tip IDs, logs, drafts — is permanently deleted. Gone is gone: erased records cannot be recovered by you or by us.

Delete by removing the app

Deleting I'm Telling from your device removes all of its on-device data with it.

Data held by others

If you handed a tip to a law-enforcement agency through that agency's own phone line or web form, that agency holds its own records under its own retention and privacy policies; contact that agency about its records. Reginal Polk Technologies holds no copy of any tip.
