Privacy Policy — SOME Politics

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

SOME Politics is a plain-language civics and grassroots organizing app — how decisions get made, how to start where you stand, and the working tools (a supporter book, a meeting kit, a 90-day calendar) to build momentum. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores — on your device only

The organizing tools keep what you type into them: your Supporter Book entries (names, blocks or neighborhoods, wards or precincts, tags, notes, and last-contact dates that you enter), your follow-up list, your “Where Do I Stand?” worksheet, your calendar check-offs, your tracker counts, and your Premium subscription state (as the App Store reports it to the app). All of it is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network calls at all — and it disappears when you delete the app. Your list belongs to you; we cannot see it, and we hold no copy of it. The app's only export is text you copy yourself, to paste wherever you keep your own records.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location. Supporter Book entries are typed in by you, inside the app — the app never reads your address book.
  • No donations, no fundraising, and no campaign payment features of any kind. The only purchase the app offers is its own Premium subscription.

Purchases

If you buy a SOME Politics Premium subscription, the transaction is processed by the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The App Store's own privacy policy governs purchase data it handles.

Children

The app is a general-audience civics education and organizing product. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
