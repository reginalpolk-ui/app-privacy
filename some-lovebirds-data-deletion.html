SOME Lovebirds — Data Deletion

SOME Lovebirds · Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc. · Contact: reginalpolk@luvvets.com

Prototype build (current)

The prototype holds data only on your device — there is no account and no server copy. To delete everything:

  1. Open SOME Lovebirds → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete everything on this device and confirm.

Every somelovebirds. local-storage key — your prototype profile, decisions, matches, blocks, reports, and any Share-a-Date plan — is wiped immediately. Uninstalling the app deletes the same data. There is nothing stored anywhere else, so there is nothing further to request.

Live service (the printed commitment, effective at live launch)

  • In-app deletion: Settings → Delete account. Deletion means deletion on a printed clock: profile, photos, and matches are removed from service immediately; message data and backups purge within 30 days, except records we are legally required to retain (e.g., safety-enforcement records for banned accounts, retained under the published retention schedule to keep ejected users from returning).
  • Web request: if you cannot access the app, email reginalpolk@luvvets.com from the address on the account with the subject DELETE MY ACCOUNT. Identity is verified before deletion; the same clock applies.
  • What deletion never requires: a phone call, a retention offer, or a reason.
