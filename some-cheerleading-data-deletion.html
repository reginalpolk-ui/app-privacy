Data Deletion — SOME Cheerleading

Effective date: November 26, 2026

SOME Cheerleading collects no personal data. There is no account, no server copy of anything about you, and nothing transmitted off your device — the app makes no network calls at all.

What exists, and where

The only data the app keeps is your My Plan worksheet answers, your tryout checklist check-offs, and your Premium subscription state (as the App Store reports it), stored in the app's local storage on your device only.

How to delete it

  • Inside the app: open My Plan and tap "Clear My Plan data on this device." This removes your worksheet and checklist immediately.
  • Delete the app: removing SOME Cheerleading from your device deletes the app and everything stored in it. Nothing remains anywhere else, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.
  • Subscription: deleting the app does not cancel a Premium subscription. Manage or cancel it in your Apple account's subscription settings.

Requests

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on request. If you have any question about this page, contact reginalpolk@luvvets.com.
