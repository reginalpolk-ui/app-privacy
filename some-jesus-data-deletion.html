Data Deletion — SOME Jesus

Effective date: November 25, 2026

SOME Jesus keeps exactly one kind of personal data: your bookmarks — the cards you tap Save on — stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage under its own keys (somejesus.). There is no account and no server copy, so there is nothing for us to delete for you — and nothing anyone else holds.

Erase it in the app

Open My Saved Cards and tap Erase my data on this device twice. Every SOME Jesus bookmark on the device is removed immediately.

Or remove the app

Deleting SOME Jesus from your device removes the app and all of its on-device data with it. Nothing remains anywhere else, because nothing was ever transmitted.

What we hold

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network calls of its own, so Reginal Polk Technologies holds no SOME Jesus user data to delete, export, or disclose.
