Support — SOME Islam

SOME Islam — "Islam, explained — its story, its book, its branches" — is the beginner's explainer of Islam from Reginal Polk Technologies.

How it works

SOME Islam is a map, not a pulpit. It explains Islam the way a good atlas explains a country: what is there, who holds what, and where the borders are argued about. Every claim in the app is labeled with whose claim it is, in three bands — THE TEXTS SAY, HISTORIANS GENERALLY HOLD, and THE FAITH PROCLAIMS. There is no fourth band: the app never asserts doctrine in its own voice, never grades one branch against another, and contains no simulated religious figure, no rulings, and nothing to sign up for.

Start anywhere: The Story (the narrative spine), The Book (a searchable explorer of all 114 surahs), The Split (Sunni, Shia, Ibadi, and the mechanics underneath), The Map (a timeline from 610 to today), or The Seeker's Path (where the traditions themselves tell a beginner to start). Tap "Save this card" on any card to keep it; saved cards live only on your device.

Why doesn't the app take a position?

On purpose. Contested communities are printed in three layers — the community's own self-understanding, attributed; other traditions' positions, attributed; and no app verdict, because the app does not keep one. The full constitution is printed inside the app under The Code, where any reader can hold the app to it.
