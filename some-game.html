SOME GAME

SOME Game Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22 · Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc.

The short version

SOME GAME collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no name, no email, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We could not sell, share, or lose your data if we wanted to — we never have it.

What stays on your device

  • Daily check-ins — just the dates you tapped "check in," stored only in your browser/app storage on this device to show your streak.
  • Saved library cards — the card numbers you chose to save, stored the same way.
  • A "seen the intro" flag — one yes/no value so the first-run notice doesn't nag you.

None of this ever leaves your device. There is no server for it to go to.

What is never stored anywhere

Your Talk Me Down conversation — the situations you pick and anything you type — exists only on your screen while the page is open. It is not saved on your device, not sent to any server, and not logged. When you close the page, it is gone. Typed text is matched against on-device keywords to route you to the right script; that matching happens entirely on your device.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, usernames, or identifiers — for anyone, including minors.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or usage-tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No data sales, no data sharing — there is no data to share.
  • No location collection. The app never asks where you are.

Phone calls and texts

The 911, 988, and Crisis Text Line buttons open your device's own phone or messaging app. Those calls and texts happen between you and those services under their own policies; SOME GAME is not part of them and receives nothing about them.

Children and teens

SOME GAME is designed for older teens and young adults (17+ store rating posture). Because the app collects no personal information from anyone of any age, there is nothing to collect from a child. The app contains scripted, curated educational content only — no open chat, no user-to-user contact, and no free-generating AI.

Institutional licensing

Programs that license SOME GAME receive aggregate seat counts for invoicing only. No information about individual users exists or is provided.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. The no-collection architecture above is a core product commitment, not a settings toggle.
