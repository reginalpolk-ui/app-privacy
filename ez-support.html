Common questions

What does EZ do?
EZ screens your organization against every IRS eligibility rule for Form 1023-EZ, then guides you step by step through preparing your formation package: state nonprofit corporation filing, EIN application, tailored bylaws, a conflict-of-interest policy, the completed Form 1023-EZ ready for your review and signature, and a first-year compliance calendar.

What does it cost?
The EZ package is $700 flat — no hourly meter. Government fees are separate and are never paid to EZ: the IRS user fee ($275) is paid by you directly on Pay.gov, and your state filing fee is paid to your state at cost.

Is EZ a law firm?
No. EZ is a formation-preparation service — not a law firm or CPA firm, and nothing in the app is legal or tax advice.

Where does my information go?
Everything you type into EZ is stored only on your device and is never transmitted. The EIN application happens with you, directly on irs.gov.

How do I delete my data?
Use the "Erase everything EZ stored on this device" option inside the app, or see the Delete Your Data page linked from the app footer.
