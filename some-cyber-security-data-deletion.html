Data Deletion — SOME Cyber Security

What data exists

SOME Cyber Security has no accounts and no servers holding your information. The only data the app keeps is your learning progress — your chosen track, completed lessons, and best quiz scores — and it is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. We cannot see it, and we hold no copy of it.

How to delete it

  1. Reset inside the app: open the Home screen and tap Reset progress. Your completed lessons and quiz scores are erased from the device immediately.
  2. Delete the app: removing SOME Cyber Security from your device deletes all of its locally stored data with it.

Email you chose to send us

If you used the organization inquiry form, it prepared an email in your own mail app; only an email you actually sent reaches us. To have a sent inquiry deleted from our support mailbox, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com from the same address and ask for its deletion — we will delete it and confirm.

App Store purchase records

Subscription purchase records are held by the App Store under its own policies; manage or delete those through your Apple account settings.
