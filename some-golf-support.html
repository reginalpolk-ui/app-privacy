SOME GOLF — THE GAME, ITS PEOPLE, AND HOW TO START

SOME Golf Support

SOME Golf is an explainer app published by Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc. — what golf is, what it really costs to begin, who was kept out and who broke the door open anyway, and which first door (municipal course, range, simulator, par-3) fits a first-timer's money and nerve. Orientation only: no swing advice, no live scoring, no booking.

Contact us

  • Email: reginalpolk@luvvets.com — a real person reads and follows up on every message.

How the app works

  • SOME Golf is fully self-contained. Everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device — there are no accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to register for.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no name, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • The People's Path builder sums a first year of golf live from the two sliders — rounds in year one and private lesson hours — so you can compare the people's door against the country-club story on the same receipts.
  • In Read the Game, tap any zone on the hole — tee, fairway, sand, water, rough, green & flag — to learn what it is and why it makes a hole hard or kind.

Troubleshooting

  • If a section ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all content is bundled in the app and needs no connection.
  • Removing the app from your device removes the app. There is no account or data stored anywhere else to delete.

For courses, programs, and institutions

  • SOME Golf's institutional licensing lane serves golf programs — First Tee chapters, parks & recreation and school golf programs, and HBCU golf programs. Write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with your program's name and size and we will follow up.
  • Report a problem with the app the same way: device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.
