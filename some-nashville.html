Privacy Policy — SOME Nashville

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

SOME Nashville is a small, human-vouched shelf of Nashville picks — from Jefferson Street to Broadway — each with a curator's why-note and a named documenting source. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores — on your device only

The app keeps only your saved picks and your shelf search/filter choices, only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network calls at all — and it disappears when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.
  • No purchases of any kind. SOME Nashville has no in-app purchases, takes no bookings or reservations, and processes no payments.

Shelf content

The picks shown are a curated shelf bundled inside the app. Each pick names the public source that documents it. The app states no hours and no prices; check the documenting source before you go. Booking information in this release is informational only — the app carries no live affiliate links and opens no links.

Children

The app is a general-audience travel guide. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
