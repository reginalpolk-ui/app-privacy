I Can't Wait Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

I Can't Wait tells you how long a train is likely to block a railroad crossing, measured from the road side. This policy says exactly what happens to your information in this version of the app: it stays on your device.

What this app stores, and where

  • On your device only: the one-tap driver reports you make (train passing / stopped / all clear, with the time), the waits you time, your favorite crossings, your saved routes, and your alert settings. These are saved in your browser's local storage so the app remembers them between visits.
  • Nothing is transmitted. This version has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no server that receives anything. Your reports power the estimates on your own device; the shared crowd layer arrives in a later version and this policy will be updated before it ships.
  • The crossing data in the app is public reference data — the Federal Railroad Administration's grade-crossing inventory via the BTS National Transportation Atlas Database — bundled into the app so it works offline. It contains no information about you.

Location — the honest part

  • Location is the point of this app, so here is exactly how it is handled: nothing asks for your location until you tap "Use my location."
  • If you tap it, your browser or device asks your permission first. Your position is used on the device to sort crossings by distance. If you used it, the position is remembered on the device only, so the list is still sorted next visit. It is never transmitted to us or anyone else — this version has no server to transmit it to.
  • The app reads no background location and tracks no movement. If you never tap the button, the app never reads your position at all — search and the launch-corridor list work without it.
  • The one outbound link — "Alternate route in Google Maps" — opens only when you tap it, and hands Google Maps a start point and a point across the tracks so it can route you around. Google's own privacy policy applies once you are in Google Maps.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data. Not your reports, not your waits, not your location. No advertisers, no data brokers, no cross-app tracking.
  • We never present an estimate as a railroad fact. Railroads do not publish real-time train data; every number in I Can't Wait is labeled a driver report or an estimate with its basis.

Paid plans

  • For drivers, the I Can't Wait core app is free — estimates, the wait timer, routes, and alerts are never behind a paywall. An optional Premium subscription for drivers is offered through the App Store at $4.99/month: Apple processes the payment, and I Can't Wait never receives or stores your payment information. Subscription status is verified with the App Store and kept on your device. Revenue also comes from community and safety partnerships and, later, fleet services.

Your control

  • Erase everything this app stored on your device any time: History tab → "Erase everything I Can't Wait stored on this device." It is gone immediately and completely, because it only ever existed there.
  • You can also clear it from your browser's site-data settings. See the Delete Your Data page.
