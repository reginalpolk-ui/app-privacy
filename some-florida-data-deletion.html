Data Deletion — SOME Florida

Effective date: November 25, 2026

There is no account data to delete

SOME Florida has no accounts, no sign-up, and no server that holds your information. It collects no personal data, makes no network calls, and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking. There is nothing to delete on our side — because there is nothing there.

What exists, and where

  • Your saved picks and shelf search/filter choices live only on your own device, in the app's local storage.
  • Nothing is uploaded to us or shared with any third party.

How to delete your data

Delete the app from your device. That removes all of its locally stored saved picks and settings. If you use your device's app-storage controls, clearing the app's data does the same thing without deleting the app.
