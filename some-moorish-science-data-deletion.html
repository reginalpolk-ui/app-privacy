Data Deletion — SOME Moorish Science

Publisher: Reginal Polk Technologies

There is nothing to delete on our side

SOME Moorish Science has no accounts and collects no personal data. Nothing about you — not your name, not your reading, not your saved cards — exists on any server of ours. There is therefore no server-side record to request, export, or delete.

Delete what is on your device

The app stores only your saved cards and reading position, on your device. To erase them:

  1. Open SOME Moorish Science and go to the About screen.
  2. Tap “Erase my saved cards and reading position.”

Uninstalling the app also removes all of its on-device storage completely.
