How Deal Diva works

  1. Pick your store on the Stores tab.
  2. Press GET MY COUPONS.
  3. Tap a code to copy it, then paste it yourself at the store's checkout.
  4. After you shop, tap 👍 or 👎 on "Did this code work?" — your vote helps rank Deals of the Day for the next shopper.

Grocery load-to-card deals work differently: tap Load to card and finish on the store's own site, signed into your store card account. The savings come off at the register.

Common questions

A code didn't work for me. Codes expire and some exclude certain items — the fine print on each card says what applies. Tap 👎 on the card so the ranking learns, and check the expiry date and the "Verified" stamp: that's the last day we confirmed the code worked.

Why does a store show "Deals refresh at launch — check back soon"? That store's verified set is being refreshed. New coupons appear the moment they're confirmed — we'd rather show you nothing than a code we haven't verified.

Where are my clipped coupons? On the My Clips tab — stored on your phone only. If you delete the app, your clips are deleted with it; there is no account to restore them from, by design.

Does Deal Diva cost anything? No. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no premium tier. When you shop through a link in the app, the store may pay us a commission through an affiliate network at no extra cost to you. That's the whole business model.

Does Deal Diva track me? No. The app collects no data — see the Privacy Policy. Your clips and votes never leave your phone.
