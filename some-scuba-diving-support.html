How SOME Scuba Diving works

Six shelves, one front door:

  • What It Is — what scuba is, where people dive, and a dive day minute by minute.
  • Start — the five steps to an Open Water card, the medical questions explained, both honest cost columns, six questions for picking a school, and a start list you tick off on your device.
  • Gear — what to buy first, what to rent first, the dive computer explained, wetsuits by water temperature.
  • Pressure — the science, plus the interactive Pressure Lab: drag the depth slider and watch pressure, air use, and the balloon respond.
  • History — from diving bells to the Aqua-Lung to today's six million divers.
  • People — the people who built this world, who runs the industry, and how this app makes money.

Common questions

Will this app teach me to dive / certify me? No — and it says so on the door. SOME Scuba Diving is orientation only: not training, not certification. Diving is learned from a certified instructor in real water. This app exists so you walk into that course knowing what it is, what it costs, and what the pressure does.

Is the Pressure Lab's math real? Yes. Seawater adds about 1 bar of pressure every 10 metres; the surface is 1 bar (14.7 psi). At 10 metres you are at 2.0 bar and 29.4 psi, your air use doubles, and a balloon is squeezed to half its surface size (Boyle's law). The lab runs exactly those numbers from 0 to 40 metres.

Where did my start-list ticks go? They live on your device only, in the app's local storage. If you delete the app, they're deleted with it; there is no account to restore them from, by design.

Why are the "doors" sections empty? Doors are labeled links to partner dive schools, resorts, and gear retailers. A door appears only when a real partner arrangement exists — we'd rather show you the guidance (the six questions, the buy-in-person advice) than a link we haven't earned.

Does SOME Scuba Diving cost anything? No. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases. When labeled partner doors lead to a booking or purchase, the partner may pay us a commission or placement fee at no extra cost to you. That's the whole business model, and it's printed in the app.

Does the app track me? No. The app collects no data — see the Privacy Policy.
