Privacy Policy — SOME Cologne

Effective date: November 26, 2026 · Last updated: November 26, 2026

SOME Cologne is a curated showcase of men's fragrances — the hottest bottles right now, the icons that built the industry, and the story of how scent became a super-industry. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores — on your device only

The app keeps only your saved scents, only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network calls at all — and it disappears when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.
  • No purchases of any kind inside the app. SOME Cologne has no in-app purchases and processes no payments.

Buy doors and affiliate links

Each bottle's page names retailers that carry it. In this release those buy doors are informational — the app opens no links. When affiliate doors are wired in a future release, SOME Cologne may earn a commission on a purchase made through a door; a commission never changes what you pay and never changes a verdict. The verdict board is never for sale, and paid brand placements are always labeled.

Catalog content

The bottles shown are a curated showcase bundled inside the app. All verdicts and descriptions are original to SOME Cologne; bottle and house names are used editorially only and imply no endorsement. The app shows no prices, because prices live at the retailer and change — check the retailer for the current price.

Children

The app is a general-audience fragrance guide. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
