Support Desk

We stand behind our products — all of them, from one desk. When you contact Reginal Polk Technologies support, a human being answers. Not a bot, not a phone tree, not a forum post from six years ago.

How to reach us

Email: reginalpolk@luvvets.com
Put the app's name in the subject line (for example, "G Code — lesson won't load") and tell us what happened and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help when you can include them.

Hours and response time

Desk hoursMonday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM Central Time
Response timeWithin one business day
Weekends & holidaysMessages are received and answered on the next business day

A dedicated support phone line is being set up and will be published here as soon as it is live. Until then, email is the fastest route to a person — every message is read by one.

Emergencies: our desk is a product-support desk, not an emergency service. If someone is in danger or overdosing, call 911 first. For addiction help right now, the SAMHSA National Helpline is 1-800-662-4357, free and 24/7. To report trafficking, the National Human Trafficking Hotline is 1-888-373-7888.

Help by app

  • G Code — lesson, quiz, or progress problems: tell us the lesson name and your device model.
  • EZ — questions about the formation package, the eligibility screener, or your document checklist: tell us which step you're on. (EZ provides formation guidance and software — for legal or tax advice about your specific organization, consult an attorney or CPA.)
  • Ole Buddy — check-in, reminder, or Scam Shield questions, and family-loop setup help. Ole Buddy is a companion, not a medical-alert or emergency service.
  • Jailhouse Lawyer — how to find information in the library. The desk explains how the app works; it cannot give legal advice about a case.
  • No Wait — crossing reports and wait estimates: tell us the crossing location so we can check the data.
  • JUST US — directory, matching, and case-packet questions. The desk cannot evaluate a case or contact an innocence organization on your behalf.
  • Rap App — contract-screening and report questions. The desk explains flags and features; it cannot tell you whether to sign a contract — that's what the report and your own lawyer are for.
  • Report a Pimp — how reporting and report codes work. Never send a trafficking report itself to this desk — reports belong in the app, in the National Hotline (1-888-373-7888), or with 911 in an emergency.
  • Start a Union — campaign setup, invite codes, and roster questions. For your own safety, never email us supporter names — the roster lives sealed inside the app.
  • Get Clean — state routing and resource questions. If you need treatment help right now, call the SAMHSA Helpline at 1-800-662-4357 — don't wait on email.
  • Wordy Birdie — sound, story, and progress questions, and grown-up account questions from parents and teachers.
  • Deal Diva — coupon and code reports: tell us the store and the code, and whether it worked — that feedback is what keeps the codes honest.

Support expands as each app launches: apps in closed testing and live preview are fully supported now; for apps still in development, the desk answers general questions about the product and its timeline as published on the Apps page.

Billing and refunds

Billing questions go to the same desk and get the same human. If a paid product ever falls short of what this site and the product itself promised, tell us — paid items are handled under the refund terms shown in that product at purchase, and disputes beyond those terms are decided by the company's president, not by a script.
