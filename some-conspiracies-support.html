SOME CONSPIRACIES — A MUSEUM OF FAMOUS BELIEFS

SOME Conspiracies Support

SOME Conspiracies is a museum of famous conspiracy folklore published by Reginal Polk Technologies. Every theory gets one complete file: the claim, where it came from, what believers point to, what the record shows, and the culture it spawned. The app presents every claim as folklore people hold — never as fact asserted by this app — and the documented record stands beside every belief.

Contact us

  • Email: reginalpolk@luvvets.com — a real person reads and follows up on every message.

How the app works

  • SOME Conspiracies is fully self-contained. Everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device — there are no accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to register for. It works fully offline.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no name, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • Eight marquee files are free and complete, and the Claim and Where It Came From sections of all 24 files are always free. SOME Conspiracies Premium (the Vault) opens every file in full, the Origin Timelines, the Document Shelf of declassified documents, and all new file drops.
  • Bookmarks and your reading history are kept only on your device.

Premium subscriptions

  • Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store: $6.99/month, or $59/year with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan. Manage or cancel it any time in your Apple account's subscription settings.
  • If Premium content ever appears locked after a purchase, check that you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple account that bought it, then close and reopen the app.

Troubleshooting

  • If a section ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all content is bundled in the app and needs no connection.
  • Removing the app from your device removes the app and its on-device bookmarks and reading history. There is no account or data stored anywhere else to delete — see the data deletion page.

Content questions

  • Every file states its claim in believers' terms, always attributed, and carries the documented record beside it. If you believe a file misstates the documented record, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the file name and the source you are pointing to, and we will review it.
  • Report any other problem the same way: device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.
