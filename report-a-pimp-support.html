Report a Pimp Support

Report a Pimp is an anonymous anti-trafficking reporting and education app by Reginal Polk Technologies. There is no account and no sign-in. In this version, reports are saved on the device only and are not transmitted to any authority, and support cannot look up a report — a report opens only with its report code + PIN on the device that saved it.

Get help now

If you are in danger, call 911.

  • National Human Trafficking Hotline: 1-888-373-7888
  • Text BeFree to 233733
  • NCMEC CyberTipline: 1-800-843-5678

Contact support

Email: reginalpolk@luvvets.com

Include the app name, what happened, and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help when you can include them. Do not include a report code or PIN in your email — support cannot open a report with them, and sharing them could put your report at risk.

About your report

Your report code and PIN are your only key. If the code and PIN are lost, they cannot be recovered. You can always make a new report.
