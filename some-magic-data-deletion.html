Data Deletion — SOME Magic

What data exists

SOME Magic has no accounts and no servers holding your information. The only data the app keeps is your bookmarks, the files you have read, and the cached state of your Premium subscription entitlement — and they are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. We cannot see them, and we hold no copy of them. The app makes no network calls of its own and collects nothing.

How to delete it

  1. Delete the app: removing SOME Magic from your device deletes all of its locally stored data with it — bookmarks, reading history, and the cached entitlement state, immediately and completely.
  2. Erase inside the app: open My Shelf and use Erase everything — two taps delete every SOME Magic record on the device without removing the app.
  3. Remove a single bookmark: open the file in the app and tap its shelf button again to remove it.

Because nothing is ever transmitted to us, there is no server-side data for us to delete and no deletion request is needed. If you wrote to our support email, that email lives in our support mailbox like any other email; to have it deleted, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com from the same address and ask for its deletion — we will delete it and confirm.

App Store purchase records

Subscription purchase records are held by the App Store under its own policies; manage or delete those through your Apple account settings.
