SOME Weave — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

SOME Weave is published by Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc.

Effective: upon publication · Contact: reginalpolk@luvvets.com

This draft matches, and stays consistent with, the privacy policy built into the app itself (www/privacy-policy.html, effective November 7, 2026).

The short version

SOME Weave collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no name, no email, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracker anywhere in this app. There is no upload, no scan, and no try-on: no tool in this app asks for, receives, or processes a photo of your face or your scalp, or any image of you at all. The few things you can save live only on your own device, and erasing them is one button, in the app, no questions asked.

What stays on your device

Stored only on your device, in the app’s local storage, under keys beginning someweave.:

  • Saved word cards — the decoder terms you bookmark (“My decoded words”).
  • A “seen it” flag — so the first-run notice doesn’t nag you every visit.

That’s the complete list. Your saved words never leave the device. There is no profile, no progress sync, and nothing about you to breach, sell, or subpoena — we can’t lose what we never had.

What is never stored at all

  • Decoder inputs. A listing you paste into the decoder is read in the page, on the device, and forgotten when you close it. It is never saved, sent, or logged.
  • Calculator inputs. Your cost figures exist only in the page while you move the sliders. Nothing about them is stored anywhere.

What we do not collect — the whole list

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account of any kind.
  • No photos — not of your face, not of your scalp, not of anything. There is no camera surface in this app.
  • No location data, no device identifiers, no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no usage telemetry — the app contains no third-party SDKs and makes no network requests of its own; every file it needs ships inside it.
  • No health information. The Scalp Literacy section is written education; it collects nothing about your health and issues no assessment of any kind.

Affiliate links

Some curriculum cards may carry clearly labeled affiliate links. If you follow one and buy from that retailer, the retailer may pay Reginal Polk Technologies a commission. That happens between you and the retailer, on the retailer’s site, under the retailer’s privacy policy; the app sends the retailer nothing about you.

Children

SOME Weave is educational reading written for adults making buying decisions. It collects no data from anyone, of any age — there is nothing to collect.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be dated, posted here, and — because there is no account and no mailing list — announced inside the app itself on your next visit. Contact for any privacy question: reginalpolk@luvvets.com.
