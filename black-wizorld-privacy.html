Black Wizorld Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Black Wizorld is a social network for everybody, owned and operated by its founder. This policy explains what information we hold, where it lives, and what we will never do with it. It is written in plain language on purpose.

The short version

Your information is yours. We do not sell it. We do not rent it. We do not give it to third parties to use for their own purposes. No data brokers. No advertisers mining our members. Ever.

Where your information lives

Every member account lives in the Black Wizorld database, on hosting owned and controlled by Black Wizorld itself — not on a rented social-network platform, and not in anybody else's hands. When you join, post, comment, join a group, or RSVP to an event, that information is stored in our own database under our own control.

What we collect — and why

We collect only what it takes to run a social network:

  • Your name and handle, so your account works and other members can find you.
  • Your password, stored scrambled (hashed) — we never see it and never store it in plain text.
  • Your bio and interests, only if you choose to add them to your profile.
  • The posts, comments, groups, and events you choose to share.
  • Your Wiz Coins ledger — every grant, purchase, earn, spend, and gift is recorded so your balance is always auditable by you.
  • Basic technical information used to keep the service secure and keep spam and abuse out.

Content you share

  • Black Wizorld is built on member content: posts, comments, photos and profiles, groups, and events. What you post for other members to see is visible to them — post only what you are comfortable sharing.
  • You are responsible for what you post. Our community standards apply to everything shared on the network.
  • Content that breaks the standards is removed, and repeat or serious violations carry the consequences described under Moderation below.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report: every post carries a Report button. A report sends the content to the moderation queue for review. A report by itself does not punish anyone — it asks a reviewer to look.
  • Block: you can block any member. A blocked member's content no longer appears for you, and they cannot contact you through the network.
  • Reports are handled under the moderation ladder below. If you are ever in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first — then report it in the app so we can act on the account.

Moderation — the points ladder, in plain language

Posts are scanned when they are saved, and violations of the community standards add points to the account that posted them. The more serious the violation, the more points: a minor violation adds 1 point, a medium one 2, a serious one 4, and a critical violation 10. Here is what the points mean:

  • 1–3 points: a warning.
  • 4–6 points: a final warning, plus a 24-hour suspension.
  • 7–9 points: a 7-day suspension.
  • 10 or more points — or any single critical violation: the account is terminated and its content is hidden.

While an account is suspended, it cannot post, comment, like, connect, RSVP, or gift coins. Member reports enter the review queue at zero points — nobody is punished for being reported; points come only from violations found on review or by the automatic scan.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We never share your information with third parties for their marketing or their own use.
  • We never let advertisers target you based on your private information. Advertisers on Black Wizorld buy placement — they never receive member data.
  • We never hand your information to anyone else except where the law itself requires it (for example, a valid court order).

Identity theft and security

We know identity theft is real, and we treat your information accordingly. Passwords are stored hashed. Member email addresses are not displayed publicly. We keep the service software up to date and access to the database restricted.

Your rights over your data

If you have an account, you can see and edit your profile information at any time. You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and you can request that we erase it — in the app, or by email; see our Data Deletion page. Erasure does not include data we are required to keep for legal, administrative, or security reasons.

Wiz Coins

Wiz Coins are closed-loop credits that exist only inside Black Wizorld. They are not cryptocurrency, are not transferable off-platform, and are not redeemable for cash. Coin balances and ledger entries are part of your account data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. The promise at the top does not change: your information is yours, and we do not sell it.
