G: GOLDEN CASTLE — LITTLE G. BIG BLOCK.

G: Golden Castle Support

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Little G. Big Block. G: Golden Castle is a vivid 2D beat-'em-up published by Reginal Polk Technologies. G, the littlest dude in the hood with the biggest heart, fights block by block through 11 stages to the Golden Castle, where King Nobody waits. This page is how to get help with the game itself.

Contact

  • Email reginalpolk@luvvets.com and a person will answer. There is no account and no sign-in, so support never needs a password — just tell us what you were doing in the game and what happened.

How the game works

  • G: Golden Castle is fully self-contained and runs on your device. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and it works offline.
  • The game is free to download. The Drip Shop sells cosmetic outfits, chains, sneakers and effects only — no pay-to-win, nothing that changes how G fights.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. In-app purchases are handled by Apple and can be restored through the App Store.
  • We collect no personal information. The only information connected to a player is purchase history, handled by Apple for in-app purchases. See the Privacy Policy.

How to play

  • Fight block by block through 11 stages — Home Block, Corner Store Row, The Courts, Mural Alley, Lowrider Row, The Inside, Train Yard, Rooftop City and Castle Hill — to the Golden Castle, where King Nobody waits.
  • As you clear blocks, your territory map turns gold.
  • Fill the Golden Rush meter to unleash G's Golden Rush.
  • Watch for The Teller, a bonus chase character on every street stage.
  • Beaten rivals join your crew. Dress G up in the Drip Shop.
  • Cartoon fists only — no blood, no gore. Light comedy, family-friendly PG tone.

Troubleshooting

  • If the game freezes or a stage won't load, close the game completely and open it again. If that doesn't fix it, restart your device.
  • If a purchase doesn't show up, check that you're signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with, then restore purchases through the App Store — purchases are handled by Apple.
  • If progress seems lost, check that you're on the same device — progress is saved on your device only.

Report a problem

  • Email reginalpolk@luvvets.com with your device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help. Tell us which stage you were on if the problem happened during play.

Content questions

  • Questions about the game, its story, or its content: email reginalpolk@luvvets.com.
