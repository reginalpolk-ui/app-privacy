Privacy Policy — Start a Union

Written for workers first. This policy describes version 1 of Start a Union, published by Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc. Before any synced or hosted version ships, this policy must be rewritten and re-reviewed — the launch gates in our runbook require it.

The short version

Start a Union v1 runs entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, and no uploads. Campaigns, signed authorization cards, and the sealed roster are stored only in your device's local storage, under this app's own namespace. We — the publisher — receive nothing: no names, no cards, no campaign data, no analytics, no crash reports. There is nothing on our side to sell, share, leak, or hand over, because we never hold it. Worker data is never sold. There are no sponsors anywhere in this app.

What an authorization card is, in this app

When a worker signs, the app stores: their full name, a personal contact (phone or email they choose to give), a typed signature, and optionally a department/shift and whether they are on the organizing committee, plus the date signed. That record is part of the campaign's sealed roster.

Who can ever see the roster

  • The organizer holding this device — and even then, names are masked by default (first initial only) in every list and view.
  • Names, contacts, and signatures become visible on screen only through the deliberate unseal action, which exists for one purpose: preparing the legal filing (the showing of interest a labor board requires). Unsealing shows a warning first, and the roster re-seals when you leave the screen.
  • Signers never see the roster. The join flow shows a signer the workplace and union name — never who else has signed, and never a list of names.
  • We never see it. In v1 there is no transmission of any kind. The app makes zero network calls.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, passwords, or sign-ins.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, trackers, or third-party SDKs.
  • No location data. No contacts access. No device fingerprinting.
  • No payment information — licensing is informational in v1 and nothing is charged in the app.

The honest limits of v1 (read this)

  • Your roster's safety is your device's safety. Anyone who unlocks your phone and opens this app can reach the campaign screens. Use a device passcode. The sealed/masked display protects against casual glances, screenshots of lists, and shoulder-surfing — it is not encryption, and we do not claim it is.
  • If the device is lost, seized, or wiped, the roster goes with it. v1 has no backup and no sync — deliberately, until a formal security review clears a synced design.
  • A screenshot taken while the roster is unsealed shows real names. Unseal only in private, only for filing preparation.

Children

Start a Union is for workers. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects nothing from anyone on our side in any case.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, sync, or any data transmission, this policy will be rewritten before that version ships, the in-app text will say so plainly, and the change will pass the security and legal reviews that gate this app's launch.
