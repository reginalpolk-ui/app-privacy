Deleting Your Data

It's Your Birthdizay!, published by Reginal Polk Technologies, is built so that your data never leaves your device. There is no account, no server copy, and nothing to ask us to erase — but here is exactly how deletion works, in full.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your Birthday Book (the people, birthdays, address labels, likes, watch-outs, and gift history you type in), your saved Birthday Plans, and your Premium subscription state — all stored only in the app's local storage on your own device.
  • None of it is transmitted to Reginal Polk Technologies or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we hold no copy of it.

How to delete everything

  1. Delete the app from your device (touch and hold the app icon, then Remove App → Delete App).
  2. That's all. Deleting the app permanently deletes its local storage — your Book, your plans, and the cached subscription state — from the device. There is no cloud backup of this data on our side, because it was never sent anywhere.

Your Premium subscription

  • Subscriptions are held by the App Store, not by us. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. To cancel, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your Apple device and cancel It's Your Birthdizay! Premium there.
  • Purchase records the App Store itself keeps are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Questions

  • Email reginalpolk@luvvets.com and a real person will help. If you believe any data of yours exists anywhere outside your device, tell us and we will investigate and confirm in writing.
