How the museum works

  • SOME House Music is a museum in an app with ten wings. The Lobby is free for everyone, forever — orientation, the origin story, and three pioneer spotlights, no purchase needed.
  • General Admission ($6.99, one-time, not a subscription) opens Wings 2–9 forever: The Birth, The First Records (with the Wall of Records), The Hot Mix 5, The Pioneers Hall, Flowers Weekly, The Voices & The Groups, The Dance Parties, and How It Spread (with the Royalties Wall).
  • Membership ($3.99/month or $29.99/year) opens everything, including the Members' Basement (Wing 10) and new wings as they are built.
  • Flowers Weekly is the museum's standing weekly exhibit: flowers for someone instrumental the industry never recognized, given while they're here to smell them. A new honoree arrives with app updates; past honorees stay on the archive shelf in the wing.
  • The Wall of Records is a living artifact catalog — search it, or filter by artist, year, or label. New records are hung as the museum grows.
  • Get This Record: the museum does not sell or host music — the labels own the masters (that is the Royalties Wall's whole story). The buttons on record exhibits open the licensed stores (Apple Music, Traxsource, Bandcamp) in your browser so you can hear and own the record from the rights holders. One door is special: Darryn Jones's button opens his own Bandcamp store, so it pays the artist directly. If a record is not in the stores yet, its button says "Coming to the record stores soon."
  • Independent: the app is a work of history and appreciation. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any artist, estate, label, or station named in its exhibits. All exhibit copy is original writing, and no photograph of any artist is used without permission.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no chat. See the privacy policy.

Admission and membership

  • General Admission is a one-time purchase charged to your Apple account; it does not renew and never expires. Membership is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store: SOME House Music Membership Monthly — $3.99/month, or SOME House Music Membership Yearly — $29.99/year. Manage or cancel membership any time in your Apple account's subscription settings.
  • If paid wings ever appear locked after a purchase, check that you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple account that bought them, then tap Restore Purchases on the Admission Desk page (top nav: "Admission").

Troubleshooting

  • If a wing ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all exhibits are bundled in the app and need no connection.
  • Saved exhibits live only on your device. To remove one, open its wing and tap "Remove from your saved exhibits." Deleting the app removes everything — see the data deletion page.
  • Report a problem by email: device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.

Content questions

  • Accuracy is this museum's building code. If you believe an exhibit is wrong or out of date, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the wing name and what you are pointing to, and we will review it against the documented record.
