Support — I'm Telling

I'm Telling is the anonymous crime-tip app from Reginal Polk Technologies.

Emergency?

Call 911 first. I'm Telling is not an emergency service and cannot dispatch help.

How it works

Pick the state where it happened, pick what kind of thing it was, and write what you know. The app shows you the right authority's own official door — a phone number or the agency's own public tip form — and gives you a Tip ID. Your tip is composed on your phone and is only handed off when you tap that door yourself. The app never sends it for you.

Is it really anonymous?

There is no account, no name, and no sign-up. The app uses no GPS, runs no analytics, and makes no network calls of its own. Your Tip ID and tip log live only on your device. What you choose to tell an agency, through that agency's own channel, is up to you — agencies' own anonymous-tip options are described at their doors.

I lost my Tip ID / my tip log

Tip IDs and logs exist only on the device where the tip was composed. If you erased them in the app ("My Tips" → "Erase everything") or deleted the app, they are gone and cannot be recovered — that is the anonymity working as designed. An authority you handed a tip to keeps its own records under its own policies.

Tell us what you saw: reginalpolk@gmail.com. Please do not email the contents of a crime tip to this address — tips belong at the authority's door, inside the app.
