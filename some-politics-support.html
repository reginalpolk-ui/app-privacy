SOME Politics Support

SOME Politics is a plain-language civics and grassroots organizing app, published by Reginal Polk Technologies. Three rooms: LEARN (what politics is, the map of offices, how a decision moves, how elections work — free and complete), ORGANIZE (a supporter book, a meeting kit, and a starter team), and MOMENTUM (a 90-day starter calendar and the counters that prove the work). The mechanics are nonpartisan — they work for anybody, for any cause or candidacy — and the app is education, not legal advice.

How the app works

  • SOME Politics is fully self-contained. Everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device — there are no accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to register for. It works fully offline.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no name, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • Your Supporter Book, worksheet, follow-ups, calendar, and counters are kept only on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. The app never reads your contacts — every name in the Book is one you typed in.
  • LEARN is free and complete, the Supporter Book holds up to 25 names free, and the worksheet is free. SOME Politics Premium (The Organizer's Toolkit) makes the Book unlimited and opens the Meeting Kit, the 90-Day Calendar, all trackers, and export.
  • There are no donations, no fundraising tools, and no campaign payment features in this app — by design, permanently.

Premium subscriptions

  • Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store: $6.99/month, or $59/year with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan. Manage or cancel it any time in your Apple account's subscription settings.
  • If Premium content ever appears locked after a purchase, check that you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple account that bought it, then close and reopen the app, or use Restore Purchases on the Toolkit page.

Troubleshooting

  • If a section ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all content is bundled in the app and needs no connection.
  • Removing the app from your device removes the app and everything stored in it (Supporter Book, worksheet, counters). There is no account or data stored anywhere else to delete — see the data deletion page. Tip: use Export in the Supporter Book to copy your list out before deleting the app if you want to keep it.

Content questions

  • SOME Politics teaches the general shape of civics and elections; rules for petitions, filing, registration, and reporting vary by office and place and change over time. For anything you will actually file or sign, confirm the current rules with your local election authority — your city or county clerk, board of elections, or state elections office.
  • If you believe a lesson misstates how something works, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the lesson name and what you are pointing to, and we will review it. Report any other problem the same way: device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.
