What SOME Lovebirds is

Seven introductions a day — the Daily Handful — then you’re done. A finite, safety-constituted dating app for adults: no infinite swipe, no coins, no boosts, no paid visibility, and safety tools that are free for every user, forever.

Current build status (honest, printed): the build in circulation is a prototype. Its profiles are simulated and labeled SIMULATED; there is no live matching, messaging, or payment. The live service launches city-by-city (Chicago first) only when its launch gates are met — a real backend, photo verification, and a staffed moderation team among them.

Frequently asked

Why only 7 people a day?
Because the deck is the problem. Research (Pronk & Denissen, 2020) shows the more profiles people evaluate in a row, the more they reject the next one — and 78% of dating-app users report the apps exhaust them (Forbes Health/OnePoll, 2024). Seven can be read properly. Then you’re done, and the app gets out of your way.

Can I get more introductions today?
No — and that is the product working. There is no refill at any price. SOME MORE (an optional subscription, coming with the live service) raises the daily edition to 14. It never sells safety, and it never sells ranking.

What does “verified” mean here?
In the live service it will mean exactly one printed thing: this person’s live selfie matched their profile photo, on a printed date. It never means background-checked or safe. The prototype carries no verification badge — its profiles are labeled SIMULATED instead.

How do I report or block someone?
Report and Block sit on every profile and every conversation — one tap. Safety-class reports (threats, suspected minors, nonconsensual imagery, extortion, requests for money) go to a human review path with a 24-hour service level in the live service. Blocking is instant and permanent from your side.

How do I delete my account?
In the prototype: Settings → Delete everything on this device — immediate and complete; the prototype’s data never leaves your device in the first place. In the live service, in-app deletion runs on the printed schedule on the data-deletion page.

Is my data sold?
No. Never. Data sale is refused in the app’s constitution, not just its policy.

Child safety standards (live service, published per store requirement)

SOME Lovebirds is an adults-only (18+) service. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. Suspected minors are reportable in-app on every profile and are suspended pending review; confirmed CSAM is removed immediately and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement as required by law. Designated child-safety point of contact: reginalpolk@luvvets.com (subject: CHILD SAFETY).

If you are in crisis

Call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, U.S., free, 24/7). If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911.
