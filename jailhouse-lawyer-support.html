Common questions

What does Jailhouse Lawyer do?
Jailhouse Lawyer provides plain-language legal information and education — rights cards, charge basics, court process, a legal-aid finder, and Deep Dive reports. Questions are answered on your device from the app's built-in library of legal information.

Is Jailhouse Lawyer a law firm?
No. Jailhouse Lawyer provides general legal information. It is not a law firm, it is not your lawyer, and using it does not create an attorney-client relationship.

What does it cost?
Deep Dive reports are $9.99 each, and Plus is $14.99/month. Purchases and subscriptions are billed through the App Store.

How do I manage or cancel my Plus subscription?
You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID account settings on your device, under Subscriptions. If you need help with a purchase or subscription, email us at reginalpolk@luvvets.com.

Where does my information go?
Everything in Jailhouse Lawyer stays on your device. Nothing is transmitted. This version has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no server that receives anything.

How do I delete my data?
Erase everything this app stored on your device any time: Pocket tab — "Erase everything Jailhouse Lawyer stored on this device."
