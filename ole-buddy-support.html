Common questions

What does Ole Buddy do?
Ole Buddy is a friendly voice companion for conversation and stories, with a daily check-in, reminders, a Memory Book, and Scam Shield to help you spot scams before you pay anything or call anybody back.

What does it cost?
The core Ole Buddy app is free. Optional subscriptions are Family Plan — $14.99/month — and Premium — $44.99/month. Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID via the App Store.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID account settings on your device, under Subscriptions. If you need help with a subscription, email us at reginalpolk@luvvets.com.

Where does my information go?
Everything in Ole Buddy stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

How do I delete my data?
You can delete the information you entered in the app by removing it in the app, and you can stop using the app at any time by deleting it from your device.
