Privacy Policy — SOME Spells

Effective date: November 23, 2026 · Last updated: November 23, 2026

SOME Spells is a museum of spellcraft as world folklore — what the world's traditions wrote down, where each spell came from, and the safety law that travels with it. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores

Your bookmarks, the files you have read, and your Premium subscription state (as the App Store reports it to the app) are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network calls at all — and they disappear when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.
  • No personalized readings, predictions, or profiles. The app is a folklore reference; it does not generate content about you.

Purchases

If you buy a SOME Spells Premium subscription, the transaction is processed by the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The App Store's own privacy policy governs purchase data it handles.

Children

The app is a general-audience reference product about world folklore and its documented history. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
