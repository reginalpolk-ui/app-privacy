What exists to delete

Very little, by design. SOME Weave has no accounts and no servers holding your information. The only data the app keeps lives on your own device, in the app’s local storage, under keys beginning someweave.:

  • Your saved word cards (“My decoded words”).
  • A flag remembering you’ve seen the first-run notice.

Anything you typed into the decoder or the calculator was never stored in the first place — there is nothing to delete because nothing was kept.

Delete it yourself — instantly

  1. Open SOME Weave and tap “Erase my stuff from this phone” at the bottom of the main page. Tap once to arm it, tap again to confirm. It removes every someweave. key on the spot — and only those keys; other apps’ data is untouched.
  2. Or clear the app’s storage in your device/browser settings (site data for this app).
  3. Or uninstall the app — the saved words go with it.

No request, no form, no waiting period, no email needed. Deletion is immediate and permanent because the data never left your device.
