Data Deletion — The Baddest Women's Shoes

Effective date: November 26, 2026

The Baddest Women's Shoes collects no personal data. There is no account, no server copy of anything about you, and nothing transmitted off your device — the app makes no network calls at all.

What exists, and where

The only data the app keeps is your saved looks and your board search/filter choices, stored in the app's local storage on your device only.

How to delete it

  • Inside the app: open Saved Looks and tap "Clear saved looks." This removes your saved looks immediately. Board filter choices reset when the app is closed.
  • Delete the app: removing The Baddest Women's Shoes from your device deletes the app and everything stored in it. Nothing remains anywhere else, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.

Requests

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on request. If you have any question about this page, contact reginalpolk@luvvets.com.
