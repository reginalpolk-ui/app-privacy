Common questions

I picked the wrong track. Can I switch?

Yes. Tap the track badge at the top of the app, or return to the Home screen and pick the other track. Your lesson progress and quiz scores carry over.

How do I reset my progress?

On the Home screen, use Reset progress. It clears your completed lessons and best quiz scores from this device. There is no account and no server copy, so resetting is complete and immediate.

What does Premium include, and how do I manage it?

Premium ($4.99/month or $49/year) unlocks both full tracks, all Foundations lessons, Penetration Testing In Full, concept checks with saved scores, and the full Careers & Certs roadmap. Subscriptions are sold and managed through the App Store — you can change or cancel yours any time in your App Store subscription settings. Organization pricing is $1 per person per month with a $99/month minimum, arranged directly with our team.

Does the app teach me to break into systems?

No. SOME Cyber Security is an educational app about a professional field. It explains what penetration testing is — authorized, contracted security testing — and teaches the profession's first rule throughout: you only test systems you own or have written permission to test. Practice is directed to your own lab and to established practice platforms.

We are a firm / city / agency. How do partnerships work?

Open the For Organizations section in the app: awareness seats, workforce-pipeline cohorts, and the plain-language briefing layer. The form there prepares an email to our team in your own mail app — or write to us directly at the address above.
