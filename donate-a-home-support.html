Donate a Home Support

Donate a Home is the information and donor-intake app for house and non-cash asset gifts benefiting two IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofits: Stars & Stripes Children Fund (EIN 33-2573003) and Eagles & Angels Veterans Corporation (EIN 88-1880660). The app is published by Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc.

How the app works

  • The Donate tab's form does not submit anything by itself. When you tap Send, it opens your own email app with your intake prefilled to reginalpolk@luvvets.com — you review it and press send there. That email is how the organizations receive your information.
  • Your form draft is saved only on your device, so you can stop and come back. Nothing is transmitted by the app, and there are no accounts or sign-ins.
  • The QR code on the Home and Donate tabs works the same way: scanning it opens your email app with a short donation inquiry ready to send.
  • After you reach out, a real person follows up and schedules a three-way call — you, the nonprofit you chose, and the realty-gift facilitation partner — before anything moves. Reaching out is a conversation, not a commitment.

Common questions

  • I started the form and want to remove my draft. Donate tab → "Erase my draft on this device." It is gone immediately and completely. See the Delete Your Data page for every option, including asking us to delete an email you sent.
  • Does the app charge anything? No. The app is free, with no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no advertising.
  • Can I donate something other than a house? Yes — stocks and securities, Bitcoin and other cryptocurrency, aircraft, vehicles, land and other real estate, and other non-cash assets. See the Give tab in the app, or email us.
  • What are the tax benefits of donating a house? Gifts to both organizations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. The app gives general information only, never tax advice — please consult your tax advisor. The full answer is on the FAQ tab in the app.
  • What does the app do with my information? See the Privacy Policy. Short version: it stays on your device until you choose to send it yourself.
