Data Deletion — SOME Braids

What data exists

SOME Braids has no accounts and no servers holding your information. The only data the app keeps is its on-device working state — the sample reviews you write in the demo, your place in the dispute walk-through, and your Premium subscription state as the App Store reports it. All of it is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. We cannot see it, and we hold no copy of it. The app makes no network calls and collects nothing. Reviews you write in the demo are never transmitted, published, or visible to anyone else.

How to delete it

  1. Delete the app: removing SOME Braids from your device deletes all of its locally stored data with it, immediately and completely.
  2. Reset the demo: on any braider profile, the review-gate demo's Reset demo control clears your on-device sample reviews; the Trust page walk-through has a Reset button for its own state.

Because nothing is ever transmitted to us, there is no server-side data for us to delete and no deletion request is needed. If you wrote to our support email, that email lives in our support mailbox like any other email; to have it deleted, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com from the same address and ask for its deletion — we will delete it and confirm.

App Store purchase records

Subscription purchase records are held by the App Store under its own policies; manage or delete those through your Apple account settings.
