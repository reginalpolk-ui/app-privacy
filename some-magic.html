Privacy Policy — SOME Magic

Effective date: November 23, 2026 · Last updated: November 23, 2026

SOME Magic is a museum and workshop of stage magic & illusion — how the public-domain classics work, when never to do them, and where the art began. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores

Your bookmarks, the files you have read, and the cached state of your Premium subscription entitlement are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network calls of its own — and they disappear when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

Purchases

If you buy a SOME Magic Premium subscription (the Workshop Vault), the transaction is processed by the App Store. The app reads your subscription entitlement from the App Store so it can open the Vault; we do not receive or store your payment card details. The App Store's own privacy policy governs purchase data it handles.

Children

The app is a general-audience reference and entertainment product about the history and craft of stage magic. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
