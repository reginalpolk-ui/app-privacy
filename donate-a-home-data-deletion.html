Delete Your Donate a Home Data

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Good news: this is the easiest deletion in the business. The Donate a Home app stores your information only on your own device — your drafts (the intake draft, and a donor-list signup draft if you started one) live in your browser's local storage. There is no Donate a Home account, and the app itself never transmits or holds a copy of your drafts on any server. Deleting them is entirely in your hands and takes seconds.

Option 1 — Erase it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Donate a Home app.
  2. Go to the Donate tab.
  3. Tap "Erase my draft on this device."

That permanently removes your drafts from this device, immediately.

Option 2 — Clear your browser's site data

  • In your browser settings, find site data / cookies for the Donate a Home app's address and clear it. This removes the same local storage.
  • Uninstalling the app or removing the home-screen icon alone does not clear browser data — use Option 1 or clear site data as above.

What about information you emailed us?

  • If you pressed send in your email app (for example, the intake, a question, or a donor-list signup), that email reached the organizations at reginalpolk@luvvets.com. You can ask us to delete that email thread and any attachments — or simply leave the donor list — by writing to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with "Delete my data" in the subject. We will confirm deletion by reply.
  • If a donation was completed, the organizations may be required to keep certain gift records (for example, acknowledgment letters and tax documentation) for as long as the law requires. Those records cannot be deleted on request, but they are never sold or shared for marketing.
