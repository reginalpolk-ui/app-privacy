SOME JESUS — CHRISTIANITY, EXPLAINED

SOME Jesus Support

SOME Jesus is an explainer of Christianity published by Reginal Polk Technologies — its story, its book, its people — for the person meeting the faith for the first time. It is a map, not a voice: every claim in the app is labeled with who holds it (the texts, a tradition, or the scholars), and disputes are printed as disputes, with every side attributed and no verdict. It is not a church, a ministry, or a devotional, and there is deliberately no one to talk to in it — no chat, no persona, no advice.

How the app works

  • SOME Jesus is fully self-contained. Everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device — there are no accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to register for. It works fully offline.
  • The app is completely free. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and nothing to unlock.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no name, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • Bookmarks (My Saved Cards) are kept only on your device, and you can erase them in two taps from the Saved page.

Corrections

  • Accuracy is this app's whole design: every card carries an attribution chip naming its source. If a card gets a fact wrong or an attribution wrong, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com naming the card, and it will be checked against its source and fixed or removed.
  • The app presents each tradition's claims in that tradition's own attributed terms. It does not take sides in the disputes it maps, and it does not answer questions of faith — it explains what is held, and by whom.

Troubleshooting

  • If a section ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all content is bundled in the app and needs no connection.
  • Removing the app from your device removes the app and its on-device bookmarks. There is no account or data stored anywhere else to delete — see the data deletion page.

Report a problem

  • Write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with your device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.
