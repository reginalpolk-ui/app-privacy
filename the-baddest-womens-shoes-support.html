How the app works

  • The Baddest Women's Shoes is fully self-contained. Everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device — no accounts, no sign-in, nothing to register for. It works fully offline.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no name, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • The board: 28 editorial picks across Heels & Stilettos, Boots, Flats, Cozy, Sandals, Sneaker Heat, and Special Occasion — plus a ranked top 10 (Baddest Right Now) and a Shoe of the Day. Every pick carries our verdict, in our words. The board is small on purpose — it is a verdict, not a catalog of everything.
  • The verdict is never for sale: no brand can pay for a rank, a verdict, or a place on the ranked board. Paid placements exist and are always labeled Featured Partner, in the open.
  • No prices stated: every pick sits in a price-door lane — Under $50, $50–$150, or $150+ Splurge — because prices change. The live price is confirmed at the retailer's door before you buy.
  • Buy doors: informational only in this release. A door names the retailer where the shoe lives (Zappos, DSW, Nordstrom, ASOS, Steve Madden, Amazon); the app takes no orders and processes no payments. When affiliate doors are wired, we may earn a commission on purchases made through them at no extra cost to you — see the FTC disclosure in the app's How It Works section.
  • Saved looks live only on your device. Removing the app removes them — see the data deletion page.
  • For brands and boutiques: the Get On The Board section lists the placement menu (Founding Partner, Featured Placement, Shoe of the Day Spotlight, Baddest of the Week Takeover, Drop Announcement Push) and an inquiry form that prepares a message on your device — it transmits nothing itself.

Troubleshooting

  • If a section ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all content is bundled in the app and needs no connection.
  • To clear saved looks, open Saved Looks and tap "Clear saved looks."
  • Report a problem by email: device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.

Content questions

  • If you believe a pick is wrong for the board — a style has been discontinued, or a door names the wrong retailer — write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the pick's name and what you are pointing to, and we will review it.
  • Brands cannot pay to change a verdict, a rank, or a why-note. Placement buys a labeled slot, never the verdict.
