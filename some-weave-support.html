Support contact: reginalpolk@luvvets.com

Getting help

Email reginalpolk@luvvets.com and a person will answer. There is no account and no sign-in, so support never needs a password or an account number — just tell us what you were doing in the app and what happened.

Common questions

Does SOME Weave cost anything?
No. SOME Weave is free for learners, always. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no paid tier.

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no sign-in. The whole school opens the moment the app does.

Does the app work offline?
Yes. Everything — the decoder, the word library, the Cost-of-Year calculator, the lessons — ships inside the app and works with no connection.

Where do my saved words live?
Only on your device, in the app’s local storage (“My decoded words”). They are never sent anywhere. To remove them, tap “Erase my stuff from this phone” at the bottom of the main page — tap once to arm it, tap again to confirm.

I pasted a listing and a word wasn’t decoded. Why?
The decoder works from a closed library and refuses to guess: a word outside the library is named as unknown rather than decoded wrong. That is by design.

Is the Scalp Literacy section medical advice?
No. It is written education — literacy, never diagnosis. It never assesses your scalp or your health, and every card ends with the same direction: see a dermatologist.

Why do some cards carry an affiliate label?
Some curriculum product links are affiliate links that may earn Reginal Polk Technologies a commission. The curriculum picks products first, never for commissions, and any link that could earn one is labeled on its card.

How do I report a problem with a lesson?
Email reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the section name (Decoder, Follow One Bundle, Methods, Cost of a Year, Scalp Literacy, Chairs) and what looks wrong. Corrections matter to us — the school’s numbers are only published where a verified source backs them.
