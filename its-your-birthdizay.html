Privacy Policy — It's Your Birthdizay!

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

It's Your Birthdizay! is a birthday book with a sending arm — a recognition service that remembers the birthdays that matter to you and helps you plan a send (flowers, cake, balloons, ice cream, anything) to a person's home or their job, published by Reginal Polk Technologies. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores — on your device only

The Birthday Book keeps what you type into it: the people you add (names, birthdays, relationship tags, home and work address labels with city and ZIP, likes and watch-outs, and your gift history), the Birthday Plans you save, and your Premium subscription state (as the App Store reports it to the app). All of it is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it disappears when you delete the app. We cannot see your Book, and we hold no copy of it. The sample people the app opens with are labeled samples, generated on the device — they are not real people's data.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, or location. Book entries are typed in by you, inside the app — the app never reads your address book.

Fees, partners, and purchases

The app's own service fee — the Birthdizay Fee, $3.99 flat — is named and shown first on every plan, and waived for Premium members. Partner doors inside the app are labeled with who fulfills each send; gifts are priced by the partner and paid at the partner's own checkout, outside this app. The only purchase made inside the app is the optional Premium subscription, processed by the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The App Store's own privacy policy governs purchase data it handles.

Children

The app is a general-audience planning product. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
