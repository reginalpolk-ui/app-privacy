How the app works

  • SOME Braids is fully self-contained. Everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device — there are no accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to register for. It works fully offline.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no name, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • Sample profiles: SOME Braids is rolling out city by city. In this release, the braider profiles, reviews, and records are clearly labeled SAMPLE profiles — real in structure, fictional in person — so you can see exactly how the verified-visit system, the right-of-reply, and the Remedy Ladder work before real braiders are seeded. No real business is listed yet, and no booking or payment takes place in the app.
  • Try the review gate: on any braider profile, the demo lets you simulate one completed visit on your device; only then does the review form unlock. That gate is the product: in the live directory, a review exists only when it is tied to a completed appointment.
  • Searching, reading profiles and records, and learning the trust system are free, forever. SOME Braids Premium opens the deep style library: a price decoder (cost per week of wear, and what actually moves a quote) and a care guide on every style page.
  • The Claimed Braider prices on the "Claim Your Chair" page are labeled proposal pricing for a future braider-side program. Nothing on that page is for sale in this release.

Premium subscriptions

  • Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store: $6.99/month, or $59/year with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan. Manage or cancel it any time in your Apple account's subscription settings.
  • If Premium content ever appears locked after a purchase, check that you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple account that bought it, then close and reopen the app, or use Restore Purchases on the Premium page.

Troubleshooting

  • If a section ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all content is bundled in the app and needs no connection.
  • To reset the on-device demo (your sample reviews and the dispute walk-through), use the Reset control on a braider profile's review-gate demo; the Trust page walk-through has its own Reset button.
  • Removing the app from your device removes the app and everything stored in it. There is no account or data stored anywhere else to delete — see the data deletion page.

Content questions

  • License labels on profiles summarize each state's braiding rule (licensed / license not required / registration) for the sample profiles shown. Rules change; for a real licensing question, check your state board. If you believe a label or a price band is wrong, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the style or profile name and what you are pointing to, and we will review it.
  • Report any other problem the same way: device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.
