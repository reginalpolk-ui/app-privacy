Just Us Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Just Us helps families of wrongfully convicted people find and apply to the organizations that can help. To do that, the app holds case information that is deeply personal. This policy says exactly what this version of the app stores, where, what it never does with it, and the choices the family keeps. The short version: in this version, your case never leaves your device.

What this app stores, and where

  • On your device only: the case information you enter — your loved one's name; the state and county of conviction; the case number; the prison or facility name; your relationship to them; whether they are still incarcerated; the type of evidence; the status of appeals; the type of sentence; whether the conviction followed a trial or a plea; whether a private lawyer is affordable; the offense and judgment date if you have them; and any notes you choose to add — plus your progress: which Case Builder documents you have gathered, the status and dates of applications you are tracking, and where the case stands on the Journey Map. All of it is saved in your browser's local storage so the app remembers it between visits.
  • Nothing is transmitted. This version has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no server that receives anything. Matching runs on the device against a bundled public directory of organizations; it sends nothing out.
  • Where a fact came from is recorded: if you scan the judgment paper (below), each field is marked whether it was read off the paper and confirmed by you, or typed. That mark is stored with the case, on the device, and shown next to the matched verdicts so you can see what the verdicts rest on.

Scanning the judgment paper

  • The scan reads official court documents only — the Judgment / Sentencing Order or a docket sheet. It proposes the facts printed there (name, state and county of conviction, case number, offense, sentence, judgment date); nothing becomes part of the case until you check and confirm every line.
  • The reading happens on your device. When you tap "Read my pages," the app downloads the open-source Tesseract reading engine and its English language data from the jsDelivr content-delivery network — that download is the one time this app contacts a third party, and it carries none of your information: it is a request for the engine, nothing more.
  • Your photos and the words read off them are never uploaded and never stored — not by the app, not anywhere. Only the facts you confirm are kept, in the case, on the device.

A note about the notes field

  • The app asks for claim categories, not confessions. Keep notes brief and factual — dates, document locations, what an organization said. Do not write detailed accounts of the events of the case into the app. The innocence claim itself belongs in the application, in your loved one's own words, as the Case Builder checklist explains.

What we never do — no exceptions

  • We never sell case data. Not to anyone, not ever, not in any form.
  • We never share case data with advertisers or data brokers, and the app carries no ads and no advertising trackers. Nothing tracks you across other apps or websites.
  • We never share a case with any organization, lawyer, or third party. Applying is something you do yourself, by the organization's own method. Just Us does not send your case anywhere.
  • Matching is never sold. Matched doors are decided by the organizations' published rules — state, evidence, sentence — never by who paid. Attorney listings, when they open, are labeled advertising and sit outside the matching.

What we do not collect

  • No email address or account is required to use the app. No location. No contacts. No photos or files are collected or kept (scan photos are read in memory and discarded — see above). No payment information is collected by us: the one optional purchase — the Case Packet — is sold by Apple through the App Store, Apple handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The purchase is tied to your Apple ID, not to your case, and no purchase information is ever connected to case data. Nothing is collected about your loved one from any outside source — everything in a case is what the family typed in or confirmed from the court's own paper.

Security — stated plainly

  • In this version, case data is protected by never leaving the device: there is no account to breach and no server copy to steal. What that means in practice: whoever holds the unlocked device can open the app and see the case. Use your device's lock screen, and erase the case (below) when you are done with a device.
  • Any future version that syncs a case anywhere will ship only after an independent security review, and this policy will be rewritten first to state its protections exactly.

Your control

  • You can delete the entire case — the case record, checklist, and application tracking — at any time with Remove this case on the Journey tab, or erase everything the app stored with Erase everything Just Us stored on this device. Because the data only ever existed on the device, deletion there is complete.
  • See the Delete Your Data page for the step-by-step.

Children

  • Just Us is for adults helping a family member. It is not directed to children, and the case subject's information is entered by an adult family member for the purpose of seeking help for that person.

Changes

  • If this policy changes — for example, when organization workspaces or a synced version open — the new version will be posted at this address with a new date before it takes effect, and the app will say plainly what changed.
