Data Deletion — SOME Spells

What data exists

SOME Spells has no accounts and no servers holding your information. The only data the app keeps is your bookmarks, the files you have read, and your Premium subscription state as the App Store reports it — and they are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. We cannot see them, and we hold no copy of them. The app makes no network calls and collects nothing.

How to delete it

  1. Delete the app: removing SOME Spells from your device deletes all of its locally stored data with it — bookmarks, reading history, and settings, immediately and completely.
  2. Remove a single bookmark: open the file in the app and tap Bookmarked ✓ to un-bookmark it.

Because nothing is ever transmitted to us, there is no server-side data for us to delete and no deletion request is needed. If you wrote to our support email, that email lives in our support mailbox like any other email; to have it deleted, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com from the same address and ask for its deletion — we will delete it and confirm.

App Store purchase records

Subscription purchase records are held by the App Store under its own policies; manage or delete those through your Apple account settings.
