Privacy Policy — SOME Dolls

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

SOME Dolls is a doll-world explainer: the kinds of dolls, the makers, the history, the collecting craft, the care trades, and the reborn culture — covered with dignity, and with the scams named. This policy is short because the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores — on your device only

The app remembers three small things, all in its local storage on your device only: your place in its three Test Drive demos (which step you were on); your collection-journal records, if you keep a journal (a Premium feature) — the names and notes you type there; and a flag recording whether SOME Dolls Premium is active on this device. None of it is ever transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network calls of its own — and we hold no copy of it. Delete the app and it is gone with the app.

What the app does not do

No accounts. No sign-up. No analytics. No advertising. No tracking. The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, photos, or microphone.

Purchases

SOME Dolls offers one optional subscription, SOME Dolls Premium, for collectors. It is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store: Apple handles your payment and holds the purchase record, and the app receives only an on-device flag that Premium is active — we never see your payment information. The app also presents a Doll Shop of physical doll clothes and accessories; those purchases are never made in the app — the Buy buttons open our web storefront in your browser, where the sale completes under that store's own privacy policy. The app shares nothing of yours with the storefront.

The directory and route-outs

Directory listings in this release are labeled sample entries shown to demonstrate the verified format. Where the app names an outside destination (an institution, a museum, a reference), it names it as text. The app's only live outward doors are the App Store (for Premium) and the developer's web storefront (for Doll Shop purchases), both described above.

Children

SOME Dolls is an explainer for adults and older learners about doll history, craft, and culture, including a shelf that explains the play lane for parents. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated first and the app will say so plainly.
