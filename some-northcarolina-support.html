SOME North Carolina — Support

Not all of Carolina, just some. SOME North Carolina is published by Reginal Polk Technologies.

Contact us

  • Email: reginalpolk@luvvets.com — a real person reads and follows up on every message.

How the app works

  • SOME North Carolina is fully self-contained. Everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device — no accounts, no sign-in, nothing to register for. It works fully offline.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no name, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • The shelf: 69 curated picks across Live Music, Food & Drink, History & Story, Landmarks & Places, and Arts & Culture. Every pick carries a curator's why-note and names its documenting source. The shelf is small on purpose — it is a vouched shelf, not a directory of everything.
  • The story: North Carolina's fuller story starts with Piedmont blues on Durham's tobacco streets and Black Wall Street on Parrish Street, where Blind Boy Fuller busked and NC Mutual rose. Charlotte answers with its Uptown museums, NoDa listening rooms and juke-joint kitchens, and the shelf still reaches Asheville and Kitty Hawk.
  • No hours or prices: the app states neither, because both change. Each pick names its documenting source — check it for current hours and details before you go.
  • Booking doors: informational only in this release. The app takes no bookings, reservations, or payments and carries no live affiliate links. Nothing in the app is for sale, and no venue can pay for a pick, a rank, or a why-note.
  • Saved picks live only on your device. Removing the app removes them — see the data deletion page.

Troubleshooting

  • If a section ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all content is bundled in the app and needs no connection.
  • To clear saved picks and app choices, use "Clear saved picks & app data on this device" on the Saved screen.
  • Report a problem by email: device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.

Content questions

  • If you believe a pick's facts are wrong — a place has closed or moved — write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the pick's name and what you are pointing to, and we will review it against its documenting source.
  • Venues cannot pay to be added, ranked, or described differently. The verdict is never for sale.
