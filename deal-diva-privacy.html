Deal Diva — Privacy Policy

Deal Diva is published by Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc. Effective: upon publication · Contact: reginalpolk@luvvets.com

The short version

Deal Diva collects no data. Not "very little" — none. There is no account to create, no analytics running, no advertising tracker, and no server receiving anything from your phone.

What the app stores, and where

Two things are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage:

  • My Clips — the coupons you choose to clip.
  • Your votes — your 👍 / 👎 answers to "Did this code work?"

These never leave your device. They are not transmitted to Reginal Polk Technologies or to anyone else. You can erase them at any time from the My Clips screen ("Erase all my clips & votes"), and uninstalling the app deletes them permanently.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-up, no login, no email address, no phone number — ever requested.
  • No location collection. No contacts, photos, or camera access.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information — there is none to sell or share.

Links to stores

Coupon offers may open a store's website in your browser. Those sites are run by the stores and the affiliate networks that carry their promotions, and their own privacy policies apply once you leave the app. When a purchase follows from one of those links, the store may pay Reginal Polk Technologies a commission through the affiliate network; that transaction happens between the store and the network and involves no data from the app.

Children

Deal Diva is a general-audience shopping app. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Deal Diva ever changes this policy, the new policy will be posted at this address before that version ships.
