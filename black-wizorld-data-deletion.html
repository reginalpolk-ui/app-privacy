Delete Your Black Wizorld Data

Your information is yours. If you have a Black Wizorld account, you may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time, in either of two ways.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open Black Wizorld and sign in.
  2. Go to your profile, then account settings.
  3. Choose Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is closed right away and your data enters the deletion process below.

Option 2 — By email

Email reginalpolk@luvvets.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my Black Wizorld data." Tell us your @handle so we can find the right account.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, handle, bio) and sign-in credentials.
  • Your posts, comments, likes, group memberships, and event RSVPs.
  • Your Wiz Coins balance is closed with the account; coins are closed-loop platform credits, are not cryptocurrency, and have no cash value.

What we may keep

We may retain limited records where the law, security, or fraud prevention requires it — for example, moderation and transaction records needed to keep the community safe and the books auditable. Retained records are never used for marketing and never sold.

Timing

Requests are completed within 30 days, and we will confirm by email when your deletion is done.

See also the Black Wizorld Privacy Policy.
