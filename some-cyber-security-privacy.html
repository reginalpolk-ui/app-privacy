Privacy Policy — SOME Cyber Security

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 20, 2026

SOME Cyber Security is an educational app that teaches the foundations of the cybersecurity field. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect almost nothing.

What the app stores

Your learning progress — which track you picked, which lessons you marked complete, and your best quiz scores — is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it disappears when you use the app's Reset progress control or delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

The organization inquiry form

The "For Organizations" section includes a form that prepares an email in your own mail app, addressed to our support team. The app does not send anything by itself and does not keep a copy. If you choose to send that email, the information you include is governed by your email provider and received by our support team like any other email; we use it only to respond to your inquiry.

Purchases

If you buy a Premium subscription, the transaction is processed by the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The App Store's own privacy policy governs purchase data it handles.

Children

The app is a general-audience educational product about a professional field. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
