Privacy Policy — SOME Braids

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

SOME Braids is the braids-only front door: a directory of braiders with reviews that can only be written after a verified, completed visit, a public record, and a remedy ladder when something goes wrong. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores — on your device only

The app keeps a small amount of working state only on your own device, in the app's local storage: the sample reviews you write in the on-device demo, your place in the dispute walk-through, and your Premium subscription state (as the App Store reports it to the app). It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network calls at all — and it disappears when you delete the app. Reviews written in the demo are stored only on your device, are labeled SAMPLE, and are never published or visible to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location. Directory search is by city and style names you type or pick yourself.
  • No payments of any kind are processed by the app — no booking payments, no deposits. The only purchase the app offers is its own Premium subscription, sold by the App Store.

Directory content

The braider profiles, reviews, and records shown in this release are clearly labeled sample content, bundled inside the app so you can see exactly how the verified-visit system works. They are not collected from users, and no real business's information is gathered or displayed.

Purchases

If you buy a SOME Braids Premium subscription, the transaction is processed by the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The App Store's own privacy policy governs purchase data it handles.

Children

The app is a general-audience directory and education product. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
