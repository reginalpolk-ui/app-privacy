SOME Cheerleading — Support

Not all cheerleading, just some. SOME Cheerleading is published by Reginal Polk Technologies.

Contact us

  • Email: reginalpolk@luvvets.com — a real person reads and follows up on every message.

How the app works

  • SOME Cheerleading is fully self-contained. Everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device — no accounts, no sign-in, nothing to register for. It works fully offline.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no name, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising, no chat. See the privacy policy.
  • The map: nine sections. Free and complete: What Cheer Is, The Truth About Getting In (with the myth wall), The Positions, Safety First, and For Programs — plus the My Plan worksheet. Premium opens Skills In Order, Tryouts, The Roads, Costs Gear & Parents, and the Tryout Checklist.
  • Safety: the app teaches what skills are and how they are learned — never stunt or tumbling technique for home practice. Skills are learned in progression, with a qualified coach, trained spotters, and proper equipment. That rule is the brand, and the Safety First section is free for everyone.
  • Independent: the app is not affiliated with any cheer organization, federation, camp company, school, or team, and uses none of their names or marks. All content is original. The home-screen photograph is a U.S. Navy photo in the public domain.
  • My Plan (worksheet and checklist) lives only on your device. Removing the app removes it — see the data deletion page.

Premium subscriptions

  • Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store: SOME Cheerleading Premium Monthly — $4.99/month, or SOME Cheerleading Premium Yearly — $49/year. Manage or cancel it any time in your Apple account's subscription settings.
  • If Premium content ever appears locked after a purchase, check that you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple account that bought it, then close and reopen the app, or use Restore Purchases on the Premium page.

Troubleshooting

  • If a section ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all content is bundled in the app and needs no connection.
  • To clear your worksheet and checklist, use "Clear My Plan data on this device" on the My Plan page.
  • Report a problem by email: device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.

Content questions

  • If you believe something in the app is wrong or out of date, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the section name and what you are pointing to, and we will review it.
  • Schools, gyms, and camps interested in the For Programs lane can reach us at the same address.
