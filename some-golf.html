SOME Golf Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

SOME Golf is an explainer app published by Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc. — what golf is, its pioneers and history, its powerhouses, and how to get started. This policy says exactly what happens to your information — in this app, the answer is: nothing is collected at all.

What this app collects

  • Nothing. SOME Golf collects no personal data — no name, no email address, no location, no device identifiers, no usage history.
  • No accounts. There is no sign-in and nothing to register for.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising. The app contains no analytics tools, no trackers, and no ads, and it does not follow you across other apps or websites.
  • No third-party requests. The app is fully self-contained: everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device. It sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data. Not to anyone, not ever — starting with the fact that this app does not gather any.
  • We never ask for passwords, payment information, or government ID numbers anywhere in this app.

Your control

  • Because the app collects and transmits nothing, there is no information about you on our servers to view, correct, or delete.
  • Removing the app from your device removes the app. That is the whole story — nothing about you exists anywhere else.
  • Questions: reginalpolk@luvvets.com

Kids

  • SOME Golf is a general-audience educational app about the game of golf. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date before it takes effect. If a future version of SOME Golf ever adds a feature that handles information, this policy will describe that feature before the version ships.
