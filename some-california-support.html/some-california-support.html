Support — SOME California

Not all of California. Just some — the some we'd send our own family to. SOME California is published by Reginal Polk Technologies.

The shelf, in brief

SOME California is a curated shelf of 67 real California places, each with a curator's why-note and a named documenting source. The shelf starts on Central Avenue, where Los Angeles built the West Coast's jazz corridor around the Dunbar Hotel, then follows the sound to Hollywood's studios, Leimert Park, Oakland and San Francisco. Soul, jazz, rock and regional foodways sit side by side, fuller than the postcard version.

How the app works

  • Browse the shelf: scroll the picks, filter by category, or search.
  • Read the story: a dedicated story view walks the shelf's arc, pick by pick.
  • Save picks to your own trip list: saved only on your device.

Something wrong?

Each pick names the source that documents it. If a pick's source is out of date or a detail looks wrong, tell us which pick and what changed — the shelf is small on purpose, and we check every note.
