SOME Game Support

Last updated: 2026-11-22

SOME Game puts conflict resolution — and a violence interrupter — in your pocket: scripted talk-downs for the fence moment, the Game Library, Penitentiary Math, and a short piece of game every day. This page is how to get help with the app itself.

If somebody is in danger right now

  • SOME Game is not an emergency service. Somebody hurt, a weapon out, danger right now: call 911. Thinking about hurting yourself: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7). Need a person any hour: text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line). These doors are also one tap away on every screen of the app.

Contact

  • Email reginalpolk@luvvets.com and a person will answer. There is no account and no sign-in, so support never needs a password — just tell us what you were doing in the app and what happened.

Common questions

  • What is Talk Me Down? Thirteen talk-downs written by people, each running the sequence real violence interrupters use: slow down, play the tape forward, do the real math, take the exit ramp. Pick the situation closest to yours, or type what's happening in your own words and the app routes you. It is scripted wisdom — not an AI companion, not a chatbot, and not a person.
  • Does SOME Game cost anything? No. It is free, forever — never a paywall between somebody and a talk-down. SOME Game stays free because institutions license it (see below). There is nothing for sale inside the app.
  • Is it anonymous? Yes — by architecture, not just by promise. No account, no name, no phone number, no location, no analytics. The Talk Me Down conversation is never stored and never transmitted, not even on your own phone. The only thing saved is on your device: your daily check-ins and the library cards you save.
  • How do I erase what the app saved? In The Code section, use the "Your data on this device" card — two taps erase your check-ins and saved cards completely. Because that data only ever existed on your device, deletion there is complete. See the Delete Your Data page for the step-by-step.
  • Can my program, school, or city license SOME Game? Yes. Violence-prevention programs, city and county offices, school districts, and hospital-based programs license the service at a per-person rate — email reginalpolk@luvvets.com. Licensing buys the service only: it confers no ownership, no editorial control, and no user data. SOME Game is independently created, owned, and controlled by Reginal Polk Technologies.
  • Is the police-stop and court information legal advice? No. Those talk-downs teach general information — how to survive a stop and keep your paperwork straight — not legal advice. For your specific situation, talk to a lawyer.

Report a problem

  • If a talk-down, library card, or math card looks wrong, email reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the section name (Talk Me Down, Game Library, Penitentiary Math, Today's Game, The Code) and what looks wrong. People act on this teaching in real moments — corrections matter.
