SOME MAGIC — HOW THE CLASSICS WORK

SOME Magic Support

SOME Magic is a museum and workshop of stage magic & illusion published by Reginal Polk Technologies. Every classic gets one complete file: what it is, how it is done, when it should never be done, and when it started. Methods are taught only for public-domain classics and documented historical material; the dangerous acts are history only, and the con games are taught so you can spot them, never run them. This is stage magic & illusion — it has no connection to the Magic: The Gathering card game.

How the app works

  • SOME Magic is fully self-contained. Everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device — there are no accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to register for. It works fully offline.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no name, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • Eight complete trick files are taught in full and free, the Code (the ethics of the art) is free forever, and the What / When NOT / When did it start sections of all 20 files are always free. SOME Magic Premium (the Workshop Vault) opens the full method and practice files for all 20 classics, the practice regimens, the Stage deep files, and the Document Shelf.
  • Bookmarks and your reading history are kept only on your device.

Premium subscriptions

  • Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store: $6.99/month, or $59/year with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan. Manage or cancel it any time in your Apple account's subscription settings.
  • If Premium content ever appears locked after a purchase, open the Vault page and tap Restore Purchases, check that you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple account that bought it, then close and reopen the app.

Troubleshooting

  • If a section ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all content is bundled in the app and needs no connection.
  • Removing the app from your device removes the app and its on-device bookmarks and reading history. There is no account or data stored anywhere else to delete — see the data deletion page.

Content questions

  • The app teaches public-domain classics and historical methods only; living creators' marketed material is never taught or exposed — that is the Code, and it is free to read inside the app. If you believe a file teaches protected material or misstates the documented history, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the file name and your source, and we will review it.
  • Report any other problem the same way: device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.
