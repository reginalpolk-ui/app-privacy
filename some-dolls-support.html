About the sample directory

The doll hospitals, restorers, and reborn artists listed in this release are sample entries — fictional listings in the app's verified format, labeled SAMPLE on every card, shown so you can see exactly what the verification standard checks (identity and premises, trade references, a disclosed-restoration pledge for the care trades; the sculpt → kit → artist → paper-trail chain, COA practice, and published commission terms for artists). No real business or artist is listed yet, and no listing in this release can be contacted through the app.

About the route-outs

Where the app names an outside destination — the UFDC, a museum, a reference work — it names it as text for you to visit on your own. In this version there are no live affiliate links.

About Premium and the Doll Shop

SOME Dolls Premium is an optional subscription for collectors (extra atlas and care depth, plus a private collection journal). It is bought and restored on the Premium screen, through the App Store; manage or cancel it anytime in your App Store account settings. Every shelf in the app is free and complete without it. The Doll Shop sells doll clothes and accessories from verified sellers: its Buy buttons open our web storefront in your browser, where the purchase, payment, and shipping are handled — the app itself holds no cart and takes no payment. Sellers interested in the verified-seller lane can start an intake on the app's Sell With Us screen, which opens an email in your own mail app — nothing is submitted until you send it.

A note on the Reborn Shelf

The reborn, culture, and doll-therapy shelves explain a real culture and report the published care literature in attributed bands. The app is not a therapist and does not recommend a doll, or doll therapy, for any person. Decisions about doll therapy belong to the person, their family, and their care team.

Appraisals

The app teaches how doll value works and prints sourced market ranges. It does not appraise any specific doll. An appraisal is a professional service — for insurance, estate, or sale decisions, hire an accredited appraiser.

Report a problem

If something in the app looks wrong — a fact, a listing, a label — email us with the shelf or screen name and what you saw. Factual corrections from the collector and artist communities are welcome and are checked against the same stamped-fact standard the app prints.
