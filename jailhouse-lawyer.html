JAILHOUSE LAWYER — LEGAL INFORMATION, NOT A LAWYER

Jailhouse Lawyer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06 (draft)

Jailhouse Lawyer is a legal information and education app. This policy says exactly what happens to your information in this version of the app — the answer is simple: it stays on your device.

What this app stores, and where

  • On your device only: your state selection, your acknowledgment of the information-only disclaimer, the items you save to "My Pocket," and your record-relief screener answers and checklist progress. These are saved in your browser's local storage so the app remembers them between visits.
  • Nothing is transmitted. This version has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no server that receives anything. We cannot see your questions, your screener answers, or your saved items, because they never leave your phone or computer.
  • Ask questions in v1 are answered on your device from the app's built-in library of legal information. Your question is not sent to any answer service, and it is not stored unless you choose to save the answer to My Pocket.

A warning that protects you

  • Never type a confession into this app — or any app. Do not enter facts about anything you did, are accused of, or are being investigated for. Nothing entered into an app is protected by attorney-client privilege. Ask what the law says, not what you did.
  • This app provides general legal information. It is not a law firm, it is not your lawyer, and using it does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Links that leave the app

  • The app links to outside resources — legal aid organizations (such as LawHelp.org and Illinois Legal Aid Online), official court forms, and the Jailhouse Lawyer YouTube channel (@jailhouselawyer247). When you follow a link, you leave the app and that organization's own privacy policy applies. The app itself sends them nothing about you.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data. Not to anyone, not ever. No advertisers, no data brokers, no cross-app tracking.
  • We never take a percentage of attorney fees and never accept referral fees. Attorney listings, when they open, are labeled advertising and never affect answers.

Paid plans

  • The core of Jailhouse Lawyer — rights cards, charge basics, court process, legal-aid finder, and the screening estimate — is free forever. Paid items (Deep Dive Reports $9.99, Plus $14.99/month, organization licenses, attorney featured listings) open at launch; this version has no checkout and collects no payment information. When payments open, they will be handled by the app store, and this policy will be updated before the first payment is taken.

Your control

  • Erase everything this app stored on your device any time: Pocket tab → "Erase everything Jailhouse Lawyer stored on this device." It is gone immediately and completely, because it only ever existed there.
  • You can also clear it from your browser's site-data settings. See the Delete Your Data page.

Changes

  • If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds optional accounts or a live researched-answer service — the new version will be posted at this address with a new date before it takes effect, and the app will say plainly what changed.
