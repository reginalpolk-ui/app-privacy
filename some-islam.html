Privacy Policy — SOME Islam

Publisher: Reginal Polk Technologies · Effective date: November 25, 2026 · Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version

SOME Islam collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs that report anything anywhere. What you read in this app is your business alone.

What the app stores, and where

The only information the app keeps is your own list of saved cards and your reading position. It is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, under the app's own namespace. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time from the About screen ("Erase my saved cards and reading position"), and uninstalling the app removes it entirely.

What we never do

We do not collect, buy, sell, rent, or share personal information — including location, reading history, or anything that could identify a seeker, a reader, or a person exploring a faith. This refusal is absolute and permanent: it is a constitutional rule of the product, printed in the app itself, not a setting that can change quietly.

The wider industry has a documented scandal on exactly this point — a major prayer app's location data reaching military contractors through data brokers, reported by journalists in 2020, and ending in a federal regulator's first-ever ban of a data broker in 2024, covering even visits to places of worship. We built the opposite: an explainer that knows nothing about its readers.

Children

The app contains educational, attributed reference content and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will be printed here with its date before it takes effect. The no-collection rule above is not subject to change.
