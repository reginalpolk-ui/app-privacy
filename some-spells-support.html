SOME Spells Support

SOME Spells is a museum of spellcraft as world folklore, published by Reginal Polk Technologies. Every spell gets one complete file: what it is inside its tradition, how the tradition practices it, when you should NOT do it, and when it started. The app presents every working as folklore and history — never asserted as fact by this app — and the Safety Law stands on every file, always free.

Contact us

  • Email: reginalpolk@luvvets.com — a real person reads and follows up on every message.

How the app works

  • SOME Spells is fully self-contained. Everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device — there are no accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to register for. It works fully offline.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no name, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • Eight files are free and complete, and the What, Safety, and When-it-started sections of all 20 files are always free. The Safety Law is free in full, forever. SOME Spells Premium (the Grimoire Vault) opens the documented practice, the sources, and the Record on every file, plus the Document Shelf and new tradition drops.
  • Bookmarks and your reading history are kept only on your device.

Premium subscriptions

  • Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store: $7.99/month, or $69/year with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan. Manage or cancel it any time in your Apple account's subscription settings.
  • If Premium content ever appears locked after a purchase, check that you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple account that bought it, then close and reopen the app.

Troubleshooting

  • If a section ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all content is bundled in the app and needs no connection.
  • Removing the app from your device removes the app and its on-device bookmarks and reading history. There is no account or data stored anywhere else to delete — see the data deletion page.

Content questions

  • Every file presents its tradition's practice attributed to its sources, and the Safety Law prints on every file. If you believe a file misstates the documented record or a tradition's teaching, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the file name and the source you are pointing to, and we will review it.
  • Report any other problem the same way: device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.
