Privacy Policy — SOME Jesus

Effective date: November 25, 2026 · Last updated: November 25, 2026

SOME Jesus is an explainer of Christianity — its story, its book, its people — published by Reginal Polk Technologies. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect nothing. Your questions about a faith are yours; the app was built so that it structurally cannot know what you came here wondering about.

What the app stores

The only personal state the app keeps is your bookmarks — the cards you tap Save on. They are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage under its own keys (somejesus.). They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network calls of its own — and they disappear when you delete the app. You can erase them at any time from the Saved page in the app (two taps), or via the data deletion page.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No third-party SDKs and no advertising identifiers.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

Purchases

There are none. SOME Jesus is free, with no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no payment surface anywhere in the app.

Children

The app contains educational content about religion. It is not directed at children, collects no data from anyone of any age, and requires no account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
