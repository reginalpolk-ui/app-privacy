Privacy Policy — The Baddest Women's Shoes

Effective date: November 26, 2026 · Last updated: November 26, 2026

The Baddest Women's Shoes is a women's-shoes-only showcase board with an editorial verdict — the baddest pairs right now, ranked, with buy doors to the retailers that carry them. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores — on your device only

The app keeps only your saved looks and your board search/filter choices, only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network calls at all — and it disappears when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.
  • No purchases of any kind. The Baddest Women's Shoes has no in-app purchases and processes no payments. Buy doors in this release are informational: they name the retailer where a shoe lives, and any purchase happens at that retailer, under that retailer's own policies.

Board content and disclosures

The picks shown are an editorial board bundled inside the app. The ranked board is our verdict and is never for sale; paid brand placements are always labeled "Featured Partner." The app states no prices — every pick sits in a price-door lane (Under $50, $50–$150, $150+ Splurge) and the live price is confirmed at the retailer's door. When affiliate buy doors are wired, we may earn a commission on purchases made through them, at no extra cost to you; that is disclosed in the app's How It Works section.

Children

The app is a general-audience shopping guide. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
