Deal Diva — Data Deletion

Deal Diva · Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc. · Contact: reginalpolk@luvvets.com

What data exists to delete?

Deal Diva collects no data and operates no server or account system, so there is nothing stored by Reginal Polk Technologies to delete.

The only information the app keeps is on your own device, in the app's local storage:

  • the coupons you clipped (My Clips), and
  • your 👍 / 👎 "did this code work?" votes.

How to delete it

Either way removes everything, permanently:

  1. In the app: open My Clips and tap Erase all my clips & votes.
  2. Uninstall Deal Diva from your device. iOS deletes the app's local storage with it.

No request, form, or waiting period is needed — the data is yours and it never left your phone.

Questions

If you believe any information about you is held by Reginal Polk Technologies in connection with Deal Diva, write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com and we will confirm in reply that none is.
