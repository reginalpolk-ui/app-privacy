SOME Cologne — Support

Not all the colognes, just some. SOME Cologne is published by Reginal Polk Technologies.

Contact us

  • Email: reginalpolk@luvvets.com — a real person reads and follows up on every message.

How the app works

  • SOME Cologne is fully self-contained. Everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device — no accounts, no sign-in, nothing to register for. It works fully offline.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no name, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • The board: the Hottest Right Now board carries ten bottles with original verdicts, and The Icons carries eight bottles that built the industry, each with its documented year. Every verdict is written for this app.
  • Buy doors: every bottle names the retailers that carry it (Sephora, Ulta, FragranceNet, Jomashop). In this release the doors are informational and the app opens no links. The app shows no prices on purpose — prices live at the retailer and change by the week, and any price printed in the app would be invented.
  • Verdicts are never for sale: no house or brand can pay for a verdict, a rank, or a kinder word. Paid brand placements (Get On The Board) are always labeled Featured Partner, Spotlight, or Takeover. When affiliate doors are wired, SOME Cologne may earn a commission on a purchase — it never changes what you pay and never changes a verdict.
  • Saved scents live only on your device. Removing the app removes them — see the data deletion page.

Troubleshooting

  • If a section ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all content is bundled in the app and needs no connection.
  • To clear saved scents and app choices, use "Clear saved scents & app data on this device" on the Saved screen.
  • Report a problem by email: device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.

Content questions

  • If you believe a bottle's facts are wrong — a house attribution or a launch year — write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the bottle's name and what you are pointing to, and we will review it.
  • Brands cannot pay to be added, ranked, or described differently. Placement buys a labeled slot, never a verdict.
