Common questions

What does I Can't Wait do?
I Can't Wait shows how long a train is likely to block a railroad crossing, with the wait timer, saved routes, and alerts so you can plan around it.

What does it cost?
For drivers, the I Can't Wait core app is free — estimates, the wait timer, routes, and alerts are never behind a paywall. An optional Premium subscription for drivers is offered through the App Store at $4.99/month.

How do I manage or cancel my Premium subscription?
You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID account settings on your device, under Subscriptions. If you need help with a subscription, email us at reginalpolk@luvvets.com.

Where does my information go?
Your information stays on your device. This version has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no server that receives anything.

How do I delete my data?
Erase everything this app stored on your device any time: History tab — "Erase everything I Can't Wait stored on this device."
