It's Your Birthdizay! Support

It's Your Birthdizay! is a birthday book with a sending arm, published by Reginal Polk Technologies. It remembers every birthday that matters to you — with reminders at 30 days, 7 days, 1 day, and the morning of — then walks you through a Birthday Plan: pick the person, pick what lands, pick the labeled partner door, send it to their home or their job, and review an honest receipt with our fee named first. Planning, recognizing the person, honoring the person — that's the whole product.

Contact us

  • Email: reginalpolk@luvvets.com — a real person reads and follows up on every message.

How the app works

  • The app is fully self-contained. Your Birthday Book and your plans live only on your device — there are no accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to register for. It works offline.
  • The people the app opens with are labeled SAMPLES, with birthdays set relative to today so every flow (same-day rescue, belated rescue, milestone lead time) is walkable. Add your own people from the Birthday Book tab.
  • The free Book holds 15 people. Premium lifts the cap.

The fee — named, and shown first

  • Our service fee is the Birthdizay Fee: $3.99 flat, on every send, shown first on every plan and every receipt — never added at checkout, never tiered by what you spend. The big flower sites add $16.99–$21.99 at checkout; we show ours first.
  • Premium members never pay the fee — it is waived on every plan.
  • The gift itself is priced by the partner you pick, at the partner's checkout. We never invent partner prices and never mark the gift up. Partners known to add checkout charges carry a warning on their door, before you tap through.

Partner doors

  • Every send is fulfilled by the labeled partner behind the door — we hold no inventory and run no couriers. Doors are labeled with the partner, who fulfills, and whether they can honestly land same-day (which depends on the recipient's ZIP and the time of day).
  • Same-Day Saver only lists doors that can honestly land today. Belated Rescue is for the day after — zero shame, maximum charm.

Premium

  • Premium is $4.99/month or $49/year: an unlimited Birthday Book, the $3.99 fee waived on every send, scheduled and yearly sends (always with an approval hold before a charge — never a silent charge), group-gift hosting, and full gift-history memory.
  • Subscribe or restore from the Premium tab (bottom nav, rightmost). Purchases are handled by the App Store; use Restore Purchases on the Premium tab if you reinstall or change devices.
  • Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple account's subscription settings.

Your data

  • Everything you enter stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it — there is no server copy. See the data deletion page and our privacy policy.
