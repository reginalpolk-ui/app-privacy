Privacy Policy — I'm Telling

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

I'm Telling is an anonymous crime-tip app: it helps a witness compose a tip on their own phone and reach the right law-enforcement authority's own official reporting channel. Anonymity is the product, so this policy is short: the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores — on your device only

When you compose a tip, the app creates a random Tip ID and keeps a small lifecycle log (when a tip was composed, when you marked it handed off, and any update notes you type) in the app's local storage on your device only. A draft in progress is held the same way. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network calls of its own — and we hold no copy of it. Erase it any time from the "My Tips" tab ("Erase everything," two taps), or delete the app and it is gone with the app.

Your tip itself

The words you compose stay on your device. The app never sends, uploads, or stores your tip anywhere. A tip reaches an authority only when you hand it off through that authority's own door — tapping a phone number opens your Phone app, and tapping an official form opens that agency's own public website in your browser. What you then share is between you and that agency, under that agency's own privacy practices.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No location access. The app never uses GPS or location services — you type the place yourself.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No purchases. I'm Telling is free for tipsters, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children

I'm Telling is a crime-reporting tool for the general public. It is not directed at children, it is not a child-safety or child-education product, and it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. If a child is in danger, call 911; reports about child exploitation belong at the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children's CyberTipline, which the app routes to like any other authority door.

Emergencies

I'm Telling is not an emergency service. If a crime is happening now, call 911 first.
