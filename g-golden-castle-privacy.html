G: Golden Castle Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

G: Golden Castle is a 2D beat-'em-up game published by Reginal Polk Technologies. It is played entirely on your device.

The short version

We do not collect personal information from you. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no location tracking, no analytics and no advertising. The only information connected to you is your purchase history, and that is handled by Apple when you buy a cosmetic item in the game. We do not track you.

What we collect — and why

Only one thing:

  • Purchase history: when you buy a cosmetic outfit, chain, sneakers or effect in the Drip Shop, Apple handles the purchase and keeps the purchase record. We use it only so the game can give you the cosmetic item you bought and let you restore it on your device. It is used for App Functionality only.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address or any contact details.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, messages or any other information on your device.
  • Any analytics, usage data, device identifiers for advertising, or advertising data of any kind.
  • Anything you type, say or share in the game — there is no chat, no messaging and no user-generated content.

Where your information lives

Everything the game itself keeps — your progress, your territory map, your crew and your cosmetic items — lives only on your device. It is never sent to us. Your purchase history lives with Apple, under Apple's privacy policy, because Apple processes the purchase.

In-app purchases

G: Golden Castle is free to download. The Drip Shop sells cosmetic items only — outfits, chains, sneakers and effects that change how G looks. They give no gameplay advantage. Purchases are handled by Apple, and you can restore them on your device through the App Store.

Children

G: Golden Castle is a family-friendly game with a PG tone and cartoon fists only — no blood and no gore. Because the game collects no personal information, there is nothing for a child to provide, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and data deletion

There is no account to delete. Removing the app from your device removes the game data stored on it. To manage or remove your purchase history, use your Apple account settings — that history is kept by Apple, not by us. If you have any question about your data, write to us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
