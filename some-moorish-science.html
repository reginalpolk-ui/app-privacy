Privacy Policy — SOME Moorish Science

Publisher: Reginal Polk Technologies
Effective: 2026-11-25

The short version

SOME Moorish Science collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs that report anything anywhere. What you read in this app is your business alone.

What the app stores, and where

The only information the app keeps is your own list of saved cards and your reading position. It is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, under the app's own namespace. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time from the About screen, and uninstalling the app removes it entirely.

What we never do

We do not collect, buy, sell, rent, or share personal information — including location, reading history, or anything that could identify a seeker, a reader, or a person exploring a faith or a legal question. This refusal is absolute and permanent: it is a constitutional rule of the product, printed in the app itself, not a setting that can change quietly. A reader researching this subject may be a person in legal distress; his questions are never a product.

Children

The app contains educational, attributed reference content and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will be printed here with its date before it takes effect. The no-collection rule above is not subject to change.
