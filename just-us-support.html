Just Us Support

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Just Us helps families of wrongfully convicted people find and apply to the innocence organizations and law school clinics that can help — most of them free. This page is how to get help with the app itself.

Contact

  • Email reginalpolk@luvvets.com and a person will answer. There is no account and no sign-in, so support never needs a password — just tell us what you were doing in the app and what happened.
  • The app also carries a Support tab with guidance on what help is free, who provides it, and how to reach the organizations in the directory.

Common questions

  • Does Just Us cost anything? The app is free for families — the journey, the matched doors, the Case Builder checklist, and application tracking cost nothing. The one optional purchase is the Case Packet ($9.99), which assembles your matched doors and checklist into a printable, mail-ready document. The same information is visible in the app without buying it.
  • How do I restore the Case Packet? It is sold by Apple through the App Store and tied to your Apple ID. On any device signed in to the same Apple ID, open the Pricing tab and tap "Restore purchase" — there is no second charge.
  • Does Just Us send my application anywhere? No. Just Us never sends your case to any organization, lawyer, or third party. Applying is something you do yourself, by each organization's own method — the app shows you exactly how, mailing addresses included.
  • Where does my case information live? Only on your device. This version has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server that receives anything. See the Privacy Policy for the full statement.
  • How do I delete my data? In the app: "Remove this case" on the Journey tab, or "Erase everything Just Us stored on this device." Step-by-step: Delete Your Data.
  • How does the judgment-paper scan work? The reading happens on your device. Your photos and the words read off them are never uploaded and never stored — only the facts you check and confirm become part of the case.
  • Is Just Us a law firm? No. Just Us provides legal information and guidance. It does not represent anyone and cannot promise any outcome — the organizations in the directory decide their own cases.

Report a problem with the directory

  • If an organization's details in the directory look wrong or out of date, email reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the organization's name and what looks wrong. Corrections matter to us — families act on these addresses.
