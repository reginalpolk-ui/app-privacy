Privacy Policy

Updated November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ole Buddy, by Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc. (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the Ole Buddy app.

What this app stores

Ole Buddy stores only the information needed to provide the features you use, such as the details you enter in the app and the settings you choose. We use that information to operate the app and to provide the features you request.

Subscriptions

The core Ole Buddy app is free to use. Optional subscriptions are available:

  • Family Plan — $14.99/month
  • Premium — $44.99/month

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID via the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive or store your payment card details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID account settings on your device, under Subscriptions.

What we never do

  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • We don’t share your personal information with advertisers.
  • We don’t use your information to build an advertising profile about you.

Your control

You can delete the information you entered in the app by removing it in the app, and you can stop using the app at any time by deleting it from your device. If you contact us, we will use your message only to respond to you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. When we do, we will change the date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
