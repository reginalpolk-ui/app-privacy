Your Data & Deletion — SOME COLD GAME

Last updated: 2026-11-22 · Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc.

There is no account to delete

SOME COLD GAME has no accounts and no server-side user data. Nothing about you exists on our systems, so there is nothing for us to delete on request — and nothing we could accidentally keep.

Deleting what's on your device

The only data SOME COLD GAME keeps is on your own device: your check-in dates, your saved library cards, and a first-run flag. To remove all of it:

  1. Open SOME COLD GAME and tap "Erase my stuff from this phone" at the bottom of the main page, then tap again to confirm. This wipes every SOME COLD GAME value on the device, instantly and completely.
  2. Or clear the app's/site's storage in your device or browser settings — that removes the same data.
  3. Uninstalling the app removes everything as well.

Your Talk Me Down conversations are never stored in the first place — closing the page is already a complete deletion.
