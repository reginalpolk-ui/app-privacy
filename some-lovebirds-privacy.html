SOME Lovebirds — Privacy Policy

SOME Lovebirds is published by Reginal Polk Technologies, Inc.
Effective: upon publication · Contact: reginalpolk@luvvets.com

Scope: this policy describes the prototype build (Nov 22, 2026) exactly as built — simulated profiles, no accounts, no server.

The short version

This prototype collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no name or email collected by us, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDK that phones home. The app makes no network calls at all. Everything you do in the prototype — the name you type, your handful decisions, matches, reports, a Share-a-Date plan — is stored only on your own device, and deleting it is one button in the app (Settings → Delete everything on this device).

What stays on your device

Stored only on your device, in the app’s local storage, under keys beginning somelovebirds.:

  • Your prototype profile — the first name or nickname, intent, coarse area band, and one-line description you enter at setup.
  • An adult-confirmation flag — that you passed the 18+ gate (your birth year itself is checked and not stored).
  • Today’s edition and your decisions — which simulated profiles were in today’s handful, and your likes/passes, so the mechanic can keep its promises (a pass is final; decided profiles do not return).
  • Matches, blocks, and reports you file — stored locally so the prototype’s safety surfaces behave the way the live ones must.
  • A Share-a-Date plan you create — who/where/when, your trusted contact’s name, and a check-in time, if you use the demo.

That is the complete list. None of it ever leaves the device. There is no server for it to go to.

The profiles are simulated

Every profile in this prototype is fictional and labeled SIMULATED on its card. Avatars are generated illustrations, not photographs of people. No real person’s data — yours or anyone’s — is in this app.

What we do not collect — the whole list

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account of any kind (the nickname you type stays on your device).
  • No photos — there is no camera or photo-library access in this prototype.
  • No location data of any kind. Areas shown are coarse, printed bands (“Chicago area · within ~5 miles”), never measured from your device.
  • No device identifiers, no advertising IDs, no analytics, no crash reporting.
  • No message content — the prototype has no messaging; its conversation screen is a labeled preview.

Deletion

Settings → Delete everything on this device wipes every somelovebirds. key immediately and completely. Uninstalling the app removes the same data. There is no copy anywhere else to delete — we can’t lose, sell, or be made to hand over what we never had.

Children

SOME Lovebirds is for adults only. The app hard-gates 18+ before any content, and this prototype is not directed at children under any circumstances.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date before it takes effect.
