Support — SOME Moorish Science

SOME Moorish Science — "The Moorish Science Temple and the movement Noble Drew Ali founded, explained — its story, its teachings, its people" — is the newcomer's explainer of the Moorish Science movement from Reginal Polk Technologies. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any Moorish Science Temple body.

How it works

SOME Moorish Science is a map, not a temple. It explains the movement the way a good atlas explains a country: what is there, who holds what, and where the borders are argued about. Every claim in the app is labeled with whose claim it is, in bands — THE TEXTS SAY, HISTORIANS GENERALLY HOLD, and THE FAITH PROCLAIMS — plus THE RECORD for dates, documents, and court holdings. There is no band in which the app asserts doctrine in its own voice, no simulated religious figure, no membership flow, and nothing to sign up for.

Start anywhere: The Founder (the two-band biography), The Teachings (the nationality doctrine in the movement's own terms), The Book (the Circle Seven Koran and its documented anatomy), The Bodies Today (who is in it now), The Map of Disputes (the 1929 succession and the classification question, printed and never resolved), What It's Not (the Temple's own words on the sovereign fringe, then what courts have held), or The Seeker's Path (where the movement's own texts tell a beginner to start reading). Tap "Save this card" on any card to keep it; saved cards live only on your device.

Why doesn't the app take a position?

On purpose. Contested questions are printed in layers — each side's self-understanding, attributed; the other positions, attributed; and no app verdict, because the app does not keep one. The full constitution is printed inside the app under The Code, where any reader can hold the app to it.

About the legal content

The app explains what courts have held about "Moorish sovereign" claims, attributed to the courts, and quotes what the Temple itself teaches about obedience to law. It does not provide legal advice, documents, filings, or services of any kind, and it never will. If anything anywhere offers you nationality documents or legal status in this app's name, that is not us — please report it.
