Not all the outfits, just some. SOME Baby Clothes is published by Reginal Polk Technologies.

Contact us

  • Email: reginalpolk@luvvets.com — a real person reads and follows up on every message.

How the app works

  • SOME Baby Clothes is fully self-contained. Everything it shows you is inside the app itself and runs entirely on your device — no accounts, no sign-in, nothing to register for. It works fully offline.
  • The app collects no personal data at all: no name, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • The board: 36 curated looks organized by age (Newborn through Toddler) and occasion (Coming Home, First Photos, Baby Shower Gifts, Holidays, Family Matching, Everyday Cute). Every look carries an original curator's note and a fabric note. The board is small on purpose — it is a vouched board, not a directory of everything.
  • Where-to-find-it doors: each look names the retailer that carries the style — Carter's, Target, Walmart, Amazon, and boutique brands. Doors are informational in this release: the app opens no links and takes no orders or payments. We state no prices, because prices live at the retailer and change.
  • Affiliate disclosure: SOME Baby Clothes is reader-supported. Where-to-find-it doors may be affiliate links: if you buy through one, Reginal Polk Technologies may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Curators pick every outfit first — a commission never buys a spot on this board, and no retailer can pay for a pick, a rank, or a note.
  • Saved looks live only on your device. Removing the app removes them — see the data deletion page.
  • Partner inquiries: the Partner With Us form prepares a message on your device and offers an open-in-my-email-app button; the app itself transmits nothing.

Troubleshooting

  • If a section ever appears blank, close and reopen the app — all content is bundled in the app and needs no connection.
  • To clear saved looks and app choices, use "Clear saved looks & app data on this device" on the Saved Looks screen.
  • Report a problem by email: device model, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help if you can include one.

Content questions

  • If you believe a look's facts are wrong — a style has been discontinued, or a retailer no longer carries it — write to reginalpolk@luvvets.com with the look's name and what you are pointing to, and we will review it.
  • Retailers and brands cannot pay to be added, ranked, or described differently. Picks are editorial; partner placement is labeled and never changes a pick.
