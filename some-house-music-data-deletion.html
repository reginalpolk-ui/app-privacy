Data Deletion — SOME House Music

Effective date: November 27, 2026

SOME House Music collects no personal data. There is no account, no server copy of anything about you, and nothing transmitted off your device by the app.

What exists, and where

The only data the app keeps is your saved-exhibits list and your admission/membership state (as the App Store reports it), stored in the app's local storage on your device only.

How to delete it

  • Inside the app: open any wing you saved and tap "Remove from your saved exhibits." Repeat for each saved wing.
  • Delete the app: removing SOME House Music from your device deletes the app and everything stored in it. Nothing remains anywhere else, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.
  • Membership: deleting the app does not cancel a Membership subscription. Manage or cancel it in your Apple account's subscription settings.
  • Record stores: if you followed a Get This Record button to a store (Apple Music, Traxsource, Bandcamp), anything you do there belongs to that store and is handled under its own privacy policy and deletion process — the app holds none of it.

Requests

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on request. If you have any question about this page, contact reginalpolk@luvvets.com.
