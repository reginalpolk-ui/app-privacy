Black Wizorld Support

Black Wizorld is a social network for everybody, owned and operated by its founder. Questions, problems, or something that doesn't look right — this page tells you how to reach us and how the common things work.

Common questions

How do I join?
Open the app and sign up — one account, your name and a handle, and you're a member. Founding Members who join during the founding window receive the Founding Grant of Wiz Coins in their wallet.

I can't sign in.
Check that you're using the handle and password you signed up with. If you're still locked out, email us from the address on your account and we'll help you recover it. Never send your password to anyone — we will never ask for it.

What are Wiz Coins?
Wiz Coins are closed-loop credits that exist only inside Black Wizorld — for gifts, perks, and the Token Store. They are not cryptocurrency, they can't be transferred off the platform, and they are not redeemable for cash. Your full coin history is in your wallet ledger in the app, and every entry is auditable by you.

How do I report a post or a member?
Every post carries a Report button — a report sends the content to the moderation queue for review. You can also block any member so their content no longer appears for you and they can't contact you through the network. How points and suspensions work is explained in the Privacy Policy.

My account was suspended. What happened?
Suspensions come from the moderation points ladder described in the Privacy Policy — violations of the community standards add points, and points bring warnings, then suspensions. If you believe a suspension was a mistake, email us with your @handle and we'll review it.

How do I delete my account and data?
You can request deletion right inside the app, or by email — the full steps are on the Data Deletion page.

Is Black Wizorld on other platforms?
The app is the platform. Anything else carrying the Black Wizorld name is a billboard pointing back here — your account, your coins, and your people live in the app.
