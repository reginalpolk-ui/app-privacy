Privacy Policy — SOME House Music

Effective date: November 27, 2026 · Last updated: November 27, 2026

SOME House Music is a museum in an app: the history of house music told where it started — Chicago. The Warehouse and the Music Box, the first records, the Hot Mix 5 on WBMX, the pioneers, the voices, the dance parties, the Royalties Wall, a weekly Flowers Weekly exhibit, and how the music spread to the world. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores — on your device only

The app keeps only two things, both stored only on your own device in the app's local storage: the exhibits you choose to save, and your admission/membership state (as the App Store reports it to the app). Neither is ever transmitted to us or to anyone else, and both disappear when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No chat, no messaging, no comments, and no user-generated content. There is no way to contact other users through this app.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.
  • No streaming and no hosted music. The app contains no audio files and plays no records.

Record-store links

Exhibits about records carry "Get This Record" buttons. The app does not sell or host music — the labels own the masters. Tapping a button shows the licensed stores where that record lives (Apple Music, Traxsource, or Bandcamp), and tapping a store opens it in your browser, outside the app. What happens on a store's own site is governed by that store's privacy policy. The app sends nothing about you when you tap; the store sees only what your browser normally shows any website.

Purchases

The only purchases the app offers are its own: SOME House Music General Admission ($6.99, one-time) and SOME House Music Membership ($3.99/month or $29.99/year, auto-renewable). Transactions are processed by the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The App Store's own privacy policy governs purchase data it handles.

Children and families

The app's content is family-safe music history, rated for everyone. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children: no names, no accounts, no messages, nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
