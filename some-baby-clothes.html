Privacy Policy — SOME Baby Clothes

Effective date: November 26, 2026 · Last updated: November 26, 2026

SOME Baby Clothes is a small, human-curated board of baby outfits, organized by age and occasion, each with an original curator's note and a where-to-find-it door naming a retailer. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It is short, because the app is built to collect nothing.

What the app stores — on your device only

The app keeps only your saved looks and your board search/filter choices, only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network calls at all — and it disappears when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind — in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell: we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.
  • No purchases of any kind inside the app. SOME Baby Clothes has no in-app purchases and processes no payments. The Partner With Us form prepares a message on your device; the app itself transmits nothing.

Board content and retailer doors

The looks shown are a curated board bundled inside the app. Every look is original editorial copy written for this app, and each names the retailer that carries the style. The app states no prices; check the retailer before you buy. Where-to-find-it doors are informational in this release — the app carries no live affiliate links and opens no links. Affiliate disclosure: where doors become affiliate links, Reginal Polk Technologies may earn a commission at no extra cost to you; commissions never buy a pick, a rank, or a note.

Children

The app is a general-audience shopping guide for adult parents, grandparents, and gift-givers. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
