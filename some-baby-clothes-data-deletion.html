Data Deletion — SOME Baby Clothes

Effective date: November 26, 2026

SOME Baby Clothes collects no personal data. There is no account, no server copy of anything about you, and nothing transmitted off your device — the app makes no network calls at all.

What exists, and where

The only data the app keeps is your saved looks and your board search/filter choices, stored in the app's local storage on your device only.

How to delete it

  • Inside the app: open the Saved Looks screen and tap "Clear saved looks & app data on this device." This removes all of the app's on-device data immediately.
  • Delete the app: removing SOME Baby Clothes from your device deletes the app and everything stored in it. Nothing remains anywhere else, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.

Requests

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on request. If you have any question about this page, contact reginalpolk@luvvets.com.
